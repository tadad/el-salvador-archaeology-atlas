# Universidad Tecnológica de El Salvador

Major Salvadoran university base for archaeology and anthropology. UTEC inherited Universidad San Jorge's programs, operates an archaeology laboratory and university anthropology museum, conducted PAHES, and publishes the journal *Kóot*.
