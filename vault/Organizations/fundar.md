# Fundación Nacional de Arqueología de El Salvador (FUNDAR)

Salvadoran archaeological NGO founded in 1996. It conducts research, conservation, public education, and site stewardship, with its most sustained program at Cihuatán. A paper's presence in the FUNDAR reference collection does not by itself establish FUNDAR participation.
