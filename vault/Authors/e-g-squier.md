# E. G. Squier

**Ephraim George Squier** (1821–1888) was an American journalist, diplomat, and pioneering archaeologist and ethnologist. His surveys with Edwin H. Davis produced the Smithsonian Institution's first major monograph, *Ancient Monuments of the Mississippi Valley*, and his diplomatic service and extensive travel made him an influential nineteenth-century interpreter of Central America.

## Contributions

Squier's [[Papers/notesoncentralam00squi|Notes on Central America]] combined geography, history, political observation, and descriptions of antiquities gathered during and after his diplomatic service in the region.
