# Carl Vilhelm Hartman

**Carl Vilhelm Hartman** (1862–1941) was a Swedish botanist, ethnographer, and archaeologist whose work helped establish systematic archaeology in Central America. During the 1896–1899 Åkerblom expedition he conducted excavations in Costa Rica and ethnographic research in El Salvador and Guatemala, later serving as a museum curator in Stockholm and Pittsburgh.

## Contributions

Hartman's Salvadoran studies documented Nahua communities, material culture, and oral traditions, including the mythology recorded in his two linked publications below.
