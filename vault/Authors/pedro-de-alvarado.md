# Pedro de Alvarado

**Pedro de Alvarado** (c. 1485–1541) was a Spanish conquistador and colonial governor who served as one of Hernán Cortés's principal captains. He took part in the violent conquest of Mexico and led campaigns into Guatemala and El Salvador, making his letters important but deeply partisan records of the early colonial invasion.

## Contributions

The corpus preserves two of Alvarado's 1524 letters to Cortés in [[Papers/documentosantigu00alva|Documentos antiguos]], where his account must be read as the testimony of a military commander and colonizer rather than as a neutral history.
