# Pedro de Alvarado

**Pedro de Alvarado** (c. 1485–1541) was a Spanish conquistador and governor who served as one of Hernán Cortés's principal captains. He participated in the conquest of Mexico and led expeditions into Guatemala and El Salvador. His 1524 letters to Cortés provide contemporary accounts of those campaigns.

## Contributions

The corpus preserves two of Alvarado's 1524 letters to Cortés in [[Papers/documentosantigu00alva|Documentos antiguos]]. The letters record routes, battles, negotiations, and places described during his campaign.
