# George Grant MacCurdy

**George Grant MacCurdy** (1863–1947) was an American anthropologist and Yale professor best known for research on European prehistory and human origins. He helped popularize Paleolithic archaeology in the United States through teaching, museum work, and books including *Human Origins* (1924), while also publishing comparative studies of ancient American art.

## Contributions

His [[Papers/jstor-659933|Notes on the Ancient Art of Central America]] examined selected Central American objects and placed them in a broader history of Indigenous art.
