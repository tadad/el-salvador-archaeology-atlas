# John M. Dimick

**John M. Dimick** was an American archaeologist and expedition patron. With his wife, he financed and directed Tulane University's first excavation season at Campana–San Andrés in 1940; he later directed the United Fruit Company's excavation and restoration program at Zaculeu, Guatemala.

## Contributions

Dimick published a concise account of the San Andrés excavations after the first season reported by field supervisor [[maurice-ries|Maurice Ries]]. The paired reports document both the expedition's organization and its early findings.
