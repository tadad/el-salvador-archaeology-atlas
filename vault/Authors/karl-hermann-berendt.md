# Karl Hermann Berendt

**Karl Hermann Berendt** (1817–1878), also known as Carl Hermann Berendt, was a German physician, linguist, and Mesoamericanist who worked extensively in Central America. He copied and preserved Maya-language manuscripts, chronicles, and dictionaries and became an important early figure in the comparative study of Central American languages.

## Contributions

Berendt supplied specialist notes on the Indigenous languages named in [[diego-garcia-de-palacio|Diego García de Palacio]]'s 1576 report. [[alexander-von-frantzius|Alexander von Frantzius]], the translator and annotator of [[Papers/sansalvadorundh00beregoog|San Salvador und Honduras im Jahre 1576]], printed Berendt's correspondence in the edition's supplement.
