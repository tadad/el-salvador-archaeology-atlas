# Maurice Ries

**Maurice Ruddell Ries** was an American archaeologist and editor affiliated with Tulane University's Middle American Research Institute. He supervised and reported the first 1940 season at Campana–San Andrés and later served as director of the Laboratory of Anthropology in Santa Fe in 1944–1945.

## Contributions

Ries's [[Papers/ries_1940|First Season's Archaeological Work at Campana San Andrés]] credits [[john-m-dimick|John M. Dimick]] and his wife with financing and directing the expedition, while Ries and his wife Muriel supervised and photographed its fieldwork.
