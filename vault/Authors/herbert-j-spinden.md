# Herbert J. Spinden

**Herbert Joseph Spinden** (1879–1967) was an American archaeologist, art historian, and museum curator specializing in Mesoamerica. His 1909 Harvard dissertation traced the development of Maya art, and his later work on art styles, chronology, and calendrical correlation shaped early twentieth-century Maya studies.

## Contributions

Spinden's [[Papers/spinden1915|Notes on the Archaeology of Salvador]] was an early English-language synthesis of Salvadoran sites and artifacts, based on museum collections and field observations.
