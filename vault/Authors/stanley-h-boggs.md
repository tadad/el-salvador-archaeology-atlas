# Stanley H. Boggs

**Stanley Harding Boggs** (1914–1991) was an American archaeologist and a foundational figure in modern Salvadoran archaeology. He directed El Salvador's Department of Archaeological Excavations from 1948 to 1954 and again led the national archaeology department from 1965 to 1988. In his final years he directed the registry of the Museo Nacional “Dr. David J. Guzmán” collection. His fieldwork, museum organization, site preservation, and publications helped establish the institutional basis for archaeological research in El Salvador.

## Contributions

Boggs first worked at San Andrés during the program initiated by [[john-m-dimick|John M. Dimick]], then collaborated with [[john-m-longyear-iii|John M. Longyear III]] during the 1941–1942 Institute for Andean Research expedition. While Longyear concentrated on the trans-Lempa region, Boggs surveyed much of central and western El Salvador. He contributed substantially to Part II and supplied the appendix to [[Papers/longyear|Archaeological Investigations in El Salvador]] (1944).
