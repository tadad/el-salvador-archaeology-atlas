# Fernand de Montessus de Ballore

**Fernand de Montessus de Ballore** (1851–1923) was a French military officer, archaeologist, and pioneering seismologist. An 1881 mission took him to El Salvador, where he investigated archaeology and earthquakes; he later developed comparative statistical approaches to seismicity and directed Chile's earthquake service from 1907 until his death.

## Contributions

His [[Papers/BSG_DELTA700FA|Le Salvador précolombien]] synthesized observations on the country's pre-Columbian remains for a late nineteenth-century European readership.
