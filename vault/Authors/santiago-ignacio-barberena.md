# Santiago Ignacio Barberena

**Santiago Ignacio Barberena** (1851–1916) was a Salvadoran historian, lawyer, engineer, linguist, educator, and public intellectual. He served as rector of the University of El Salvador and worked with national scientific and cultural institutions, while his historical and archaeological writings documented places including Tazumal, Casa Blanca, Cara Sucia, and the Corinto rock shelter.

## Contributions

The first volume of his [[Papers/historiadeelsalv01barb|Historia de El Salvador]], preserved in this corpus, joined political history with geography, ethnography, and observations on the country's archaeological past.
