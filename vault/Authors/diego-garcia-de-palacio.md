# Diego García de Palacio

**Diego García de Palacio** (c. 1540–1595) was a Spanish colonial magistrate, administrator, and writer. As an *oidor* of the Audiencia of Guatemala, he prepared a detailed 1576 report on the provinces of Guatemala, San Salvador, and Honduras that recorded Indigenous communities and customs and included one of the earliest European descriptions of the ruins of Copán.

## Contributions

His 1576 report survives here through [[Papers/sansalvadorundh00beregoog|San Salvador und Honduras im Jahre 1576]], a German edition translated and annotated by [[alexander-von-frantzius|Alexander von Frantzius]], with supplementary language notes supplied by [[karl-hermann-berendt|Karl Hermann Berendt]].
