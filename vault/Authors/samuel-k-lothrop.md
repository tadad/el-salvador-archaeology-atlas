# Samuel K. Lothrop

**Samuel Kirkland Lothrop** (1892–1965) was an American archaeologist associated for much of his career with Harvard's Peabody Museum. His fieldwork and publications ranged across Central and South America, with influential studies of ceramics, metallurgy, goldwork, and Costa Rica's stone spheres.

## Contributions

Lothrop led the Museum of the American Indian's 1925–1926 Central American expedition and worked in El Salvador with [[jorge-larde-y-arthes|Jorge Lardé y Arthés]]. His Salvadoran publications treated living pottery production, ceramic chronology, archaeological reconnaissance, and the southeastern edge of the Maya world.
