# Maya-related

Material and settlement traditions and historical or ethnolinguistic references discussed within the southeastern Maya sphere. The shared record does not imply that material culture and ethnic identity are interchangeable; each relationship requires source-specific evidence.

## Sources

- [[Papers/larde-1921-poblacion-el-salvador#Page 9|Lardé’s 1921 population study, PDF pp. 9–17]] presents an early culture-history synthesis and assigns several Salvadoran sites to proposed Maya migrations. Its transoceanic origin theories, chronology, and direct equations of language, material culture, and ethnicity are obsolete; the value here is historiographic and bibliographic.
