# Maritime / Industrial

Historic shipping, machinery, port, and industrial material. This broad atlas category groups related modern archaeological evidence without asserting a single culture.

## Indigo-production context

- [[Papers/anil_mozino#Page 10|Moziño’s indigo treatise and editorial dossier, PDF pp. 10–13]] says he traveled through the provinces of San Salvador, San Vicente, and Zacatecoluca before presenting his 1797 processing report. It supplies regional technological context for historic indigo works, not site-specific field evidence.
- [[Papers/anil_guevara#Page 15|Clara de Guevara’s 1976 Chalatenango study, PDF pp. 15–16 and 33–35]] maps active indigo workers around Arcatao and documents artisanal vats and processing. It is a modern ethnographic comparator, not proof that an excavated colonial installation used identical practices.
