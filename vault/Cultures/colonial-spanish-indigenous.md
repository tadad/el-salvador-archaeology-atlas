# Colonial Spanish / Indigenous

Colonial contexts produced through interaction among Spanish, Indigenous, African, and mixed communities. More specific identities belong in Place narratives only when supported by evidence.

## Contextual sources

- [[Papers/sansalvadorundh00beregoog#Page 16|García de Palacio’s 1576 report in the 1873 German edition, PDF pp. 16–24]] describes the Izalcos, Acajutla, Apaneca, Ahuachapán, Coatepeque, Güija, and other colonial localities. It is a colonial administrative account filtered through a later edition, not transparent evidence of Indigenous identity.
- [[Papers/borhegyi_esquipulas_1954#Page 14|Borhegyi’s 1954 Esquipulas-cult appendix, PDF p. 14]] heads its El Salvador list as eleven localities but visibly enumerates ten, including Atiquizaya, as places where the cult then existed. The discrepancy is in the source, and the scan preserves the Salvadoran column even though the original automatic extraction dropped it. The list is religious-history context, not an archaeological culture assignment.
