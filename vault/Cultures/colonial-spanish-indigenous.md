# Colonial Spanish / Indigenous

Archaeological contexts dating to Spanish rule, broadly 1524–1821. Place records use more specific cultural classifications when supported by their sources.

## Contextual sources

- [[Papers/sansalvadorundh00beregoog#Page 16|García de Palacio’s 1576 report in the 1873 German edition, PDF pp. 16–24]] describes the Izalcos, Acajutla, Apaneca, Ahuachapán, Coatepeque, Güija, and other localities under Spanish administration. The record supplies historical and geographic context through a later German edition.
- [[Papers/borhegyi_esquipulas_1954#Page 14|Borhegyi’s 1954 Esquipulas-cult appendix, PDF p. 14]] heads its El Salvador list as eleven localities but visibly enumerates ten, including Atiquizaya, as places where the cult then existed. The discrepancy is in the source, and the scan preserves the Salvadoran column even though the original automatic extraction dropped it. The list is religious-history context, not an archaeological culture assignment.
