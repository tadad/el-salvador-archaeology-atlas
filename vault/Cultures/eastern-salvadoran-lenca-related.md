# Eastern Salvadoran / Lenca-related

Eastern Salvadoran archaeological traditions and historical or ethnolinguistic references discussed by their sources in relation to Lenca populations. The shared record does not imply that material culture and ethnic identity are interchangeable; each relationship requires source-specific evidence.

## Sources

- [[Papers/larde-1921-poblacion-el-salvador#Page 18|Lardé’s 1921 population study, PDF pp. 18–20]] reviews conflicting identifications of “chontal,” Ch’orti’, and Lenca and proposes a broad northern/eastern Salvadoran distribution. The discussion preserves useful references to earlier sources, but its language-family groupings and ethnic boundaries require modern verification.
