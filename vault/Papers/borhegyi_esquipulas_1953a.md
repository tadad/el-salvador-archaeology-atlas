Don Bernardo Abeyta's application for the permission to build
a chapel in Potrero traveled quickly through the proper channels
and reached Durango, the seat of the archbishop, from where the
parishes in New Mexico had been administered since 1797. From

The Reverand Father Fray José Andrés Corea, who evidently
undertook the expense of the door as his devout contribution, was
the custodian of the parish of Santa Cruz between the years
1814 and 1818. No information was available on Pedro Domín-
guez, the woodcarver, but it is known that members of the
Domínguez family in Las Truchas have been noted woodcarvers
for many generations. On May 8, 1818, the Ecclesiastical Gov-
ernor and Visitador General from Durango, the Reverend Juan
Bautista Ladrón de Guevara, paid a visit to the Santuario, which
was already in use at that time (Document 10).
