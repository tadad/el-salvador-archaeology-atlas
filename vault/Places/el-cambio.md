# El Cambio

## Overview

An excavated site in San Juan Opico, in the Zapotitán valley, with documented funerary contexts.

## Chronology

The compiled bibliography names Preclassic burials; a later article reports one excavated individual as Early Postclassic (900–1250 CE).

## How it was investigated

A 2006–2007 excavation recorded burial contexts, positions, funerary pattern, and associated objects. A 2008 osteological study examined remains from the excavations.

## What archaeologists found

The sources describe burials and associated objects but do not provide a complete catalog. One later-studied cranium had intentional tabular-erect shaping.

## Research history

### 2021 — Collection analysis

An El Cambio cranium was photographed, modeled through photogrammetry, and facially approximated.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 268|identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 268–274]]

### 2011 — Reinterpretation

A tephrostratigraphic study reconsidered volcanic effects and the site's archaeological context.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 39|anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 39–40]]

### 2009 — Collection analysis

A thesis reanalyzed Burial EC I 8A and its funerary context.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 54|anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 54, 59]]

### 2008 — Collection analysis

The excavated burial remains received osteological analysis.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 54|anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 54, 59]]

### 2006–2007 — Fieldwork

The archaeological project excavated the site and its burials.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 54|anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 54, 59]]
