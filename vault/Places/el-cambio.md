# El Cambio

## Description

An excavated site in San Juan Opico, in the Zapotitán valley, with documented funerary contexts. The sources describe burials and associated objects but do not provide a complete catalog. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 54|Source: PDF pp. 54, 59 (printed pp. 54, 59), Table 2 and review of El Cambio burial work]]

## Studies

### 2021: Collection analysis

An El Cambio cranium was photographed, modeled through photogrammetry, and facially approximated. One later-studied cranium had intentional tabular-erect shaping. [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 268|Source: identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 268–274]]

### 2011: Reinterpretation

A tephrostratigraphic study reconsidered volcanic effects and the site's archaeological context. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 39|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 39–40]]

### 2009: Collection analysis

A thesis reanalyzed Burial EC I 8A and its funerary context. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 54|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 54, 59]]

### 2008: Collection analysis

The excavated burial remains received osteological analysis. A 2008 osteological study examined remains from the excavations. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 54|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 54, 59]]

### 2006–2007: Fieldwork

The archaeological project excavated the site and its burials. A 2006–2007 excavation recorded burial contexts, positions, funerary pattern, and associated objects. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 54|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 54, 59]]
