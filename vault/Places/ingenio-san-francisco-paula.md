# Ingenio San Francisco de Paula / El Brujo

## Description

The remains of a small ironworks north of Metapán, also known as El Brujo. Documentary records from 1807–1811 mention its construction; it was recorded as ruined by 1910. The survey recorded foundations, walls, columns, retaining walls, water channels, a water basin associated with a wooden wheel and a masonry aqueduct bridge. [Source: PDF p. 4 (printed p. 842), "Ingenio San Francisco de Paula o El Brujo"; PDF p. 12 (printed p. 850), fig. 2](https://www.mesoweb.com/Simposio/pdf/23/Erquicia.2010.pdf#page=4)
