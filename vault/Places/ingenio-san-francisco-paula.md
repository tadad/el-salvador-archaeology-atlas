# Ingenio San Francisco de Paula / El Brujo

## Overview

The remains of a small ironworks north of Metapán, also known as El Brujo.

## Chronology

Documentary records from 1807–1811 mention its construction; it was recorded as ruined by 1910.

## How it was investigated

PAHES documented the site through field visits, photography, location recording, a measured preliminary plan and historical research.

## What archaeologists found

The survey recorded foundations, walls, columns, retaining walls, water channels, a water basin associated with a wooden wheel and a masonry aqueduct bridge. The main ruin was being reused as a cattle stable.

## Research history
