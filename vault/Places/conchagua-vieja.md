# Conchagua Vieja

## Description

A ridge site on northeastern Isla Conchagüita with numerous stone house mounds and the standing walls of a colonial church. The church was in use by June 22, 1586. The other remains are undated; the report only describes some painted pottery as evidence of an earlier culture. The report describes stone house mounds, church walls, a plaza with a cross-stand, and surface potsherds, metates, and obsidian blades. [[Papers/longyear#Page 16|Source: PDF p. 16 and pp. 18–19 (printed pp. 9 and 11–12), Part II survey introduction and ‘Conchagua Vieja’ entry]]

## Studies

### 2002, 2003, and 2005: Fieldwork

Gómez investigated prehistoric and historic sites on Conchagüita and neighboring Gulf islands; the synthesis does not assign this named site to a particular season. [Source: Martínez Rosales thesis, PDF p. 20](https://biblioteca.utec.edu.sv/siab/virtual/tesis/87502.pdf#page=20)

### 1941–1942: Site recording

The Longyear expedition recorded Conchagua Vieja during its eastern reconnaissance. Longyear visited and studied the site during the report’s fall–winter 1941–42 reconnaissance, describing visible ruins and surface artifacts. No controlled excavation is documented. The report describes stone house mounds, church walls, a plaza with a cross-stand, and surface potsherds, metates, and obsidian blades. Sherds and obsidian were also visible in trail banks near the beach. [[Papers/longyear#Page 16|Source: longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 16, 18–19]]
