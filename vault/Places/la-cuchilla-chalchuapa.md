# La Cuchilla, Chalchuapa

## Description

Three test pits documented cultural strata about one metre deep, abundant material, and a secondary Late Preclassic burial with obsidian blades and six vessels. [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 105|Source: PDF p. 105]]
