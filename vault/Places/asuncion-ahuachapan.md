# Nuestra Señora de la Asunción, Ahuachapán

## Description

Excavation inside and around this colonial church documented Late Preclassic deposits beneath the church and its later fill. The lower deposit is dated to the Late Preclassic, 400 BCE–250 CE. They recorded 26 burials. [[Papers/asuncion_ahuachapan#Page 3|Source: PDF pp. 3–7, 15 (printed pp. 1–5, 13), introduction, methods, stratigraphy, burials, and ceramics]]

## Studies

### 2002–2003: Fieldwork

A later rescue campaign documented architectural remains, weapons, and ammunition at the church. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 7|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 433)]]

### October 1985: Fieldwork

A ten-day project opened twelve test pits. Archaeologists excavated 12 test pits in the nave and outside the church to examine the subsurface, reported cavities, and foundation details. They recorded 26 burials. A compact lower layer contained Late Preclassic ceramics and possibly two constructions; overlying church fill contained historic burials and some Protohistoric and Historic ceramics. [[Papers/asuncion_ahuachapan#Page 3|Source: asuncion_ahuachapan.pdf, PDF pp. 3–7]]
