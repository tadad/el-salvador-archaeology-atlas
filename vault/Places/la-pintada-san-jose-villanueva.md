# La Pintada, San José Villanueva

## Description

A volcanic-tuff rock shelter above a small quebrada carries a dense field of petroglyphs. Researchers made scaled photographs and drawings and conducted a walkover. [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 117|Source: PDF p. 117–118]]
