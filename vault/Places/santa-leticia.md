# Santa Leticia

## Description

A pre-Hispanic archaeological site in the Cordillera Apaneca-Ilamatepec. A 1977 project investigated Santa Leticia through reconnaissance and excavation. The cited synthesis dates the site’s ‘potbellied’ sculptures to the Late Preclassic but does not date the site’s full occupation. The sources report Late Preclassic ‘potbellied’ sculptures and fragmentary Bolinas-type figurines. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 4|Source: PDF p. 4 (printed p. 430), 1970s research history]] [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 3|Source: PDF p. 3 (printed p. 782), regional history of Bolinas-type figurine finds]]

## Studies

### 2024: Reinterpretation

Santa Leticia fragments were incorporated into a revised comparative model of Bolinas figurines; no renewed excavation is reported. The sources report Late Preclassic ‘potbellied’ sculptures and fragmentary Bolinas-type figurines. The latter came mainly from surface, structural-fill, mixed, or secondary deposits; no specimen inventory or excavation-unit context is given. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 1|Source: 2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF pp. 1–3]]

### 1977: Fieldwork

The project combined regional reconnaissance, topographic mapping, and excavation to establish a ceramic and chronological sequence. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 4|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 4 (printed p. 430)]]
