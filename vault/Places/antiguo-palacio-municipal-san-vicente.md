# Former Palacio Municipal, San Vicente

## Description

Archaeology below the former municipal palace documented floors, paving, and foundations from successive buildings in San Vicente's civic center. Test pits exposed tiles in 2018; rescue excavations in 2019 and 2023 identified remains attributed to nineteenth- and twentieth-century phases, including a plaque marked “Marin 1891.” [[Papers/2023-flores-bajo-tempisque-san-vicente#Page 2|Source: PDF p. 2, discovery and project background]] [Source: Ministry of Culture, 20 April 2024](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/dan-a-conocer-hallazgos-arqueologicos-en-el-antiguo-palacio-municipal-de-san-vicente/)

## Studies

### 2018–2023: Inspection and rescue archaeology

The published study reconstructs the municipal parcel's architectural history and the Ministry notice reports the excavated sequence: fired-clay and stone surfaces, masonry, adobe, later cement floors, and domestic ceramics. [[Papers/2023-flores-bajo-tempisque-san-vicente#Page 9|Source: PDF pp. 9–19, historic plans, observations, and archaeological interpretation]] [Source: Ministry of Culture, 20 April 2024](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/dan-a-conocer-hallazgos-arqueologicos-en-el-antiguo-palacio-municipal-de-san-vicente/)
