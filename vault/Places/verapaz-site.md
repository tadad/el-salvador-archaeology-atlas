# Verapaz archaeological site

## Description

A Río Verapaz landslide exposed a Middle Preclassic settlement. Rescue work recorded a collective burial of at least two adults and infants with more than twenty-two ceramic offerings and lithic objects. [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 83|Source: PDF p. 83–84]]
