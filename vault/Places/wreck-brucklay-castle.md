# Brucklay Castle

## Description

A sailing ship reported wrecked off Jiquilisco Bay whose physical remains have not been located securely. Built in Scotland in 1867 and wrecked in 1896. No confirmed archaeological remains can be assigned to Brucklay Castle in the local report. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 15|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF pp. 15, 17–18, and 39]]

## Studies

### 2013–2014: Unsuccessful search and report

Researchers sought Brucklay Castle in 2013 but investigated another vessel. The 2014 report states that a visit to Brucklay Castle remained pending. A 2013 search used interviews and dives, but the wreck encountered was a steamship later identified as SS Honduras, not Brucklay Castle. No confirmed archaeological remains can be assigned to Brucklay Castle in the local report. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 15|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF pp. 15, 17–18, and 39]]
