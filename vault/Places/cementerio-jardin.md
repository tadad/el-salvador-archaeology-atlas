# Cementerio Jardín

## Overview

A salvage-excavated locality about 700 m southeast of the main Tazumal group.

## Chronology

Surface material includes Late Preclassic, Late Classic and Early Postclassic remains; the excavated building belongs to the Early Postclassic component.

## How it was investigated

In 1985, a salvage project used test-unit transects and then exposed 80 m² around stone-and-adobe remains. Parts of the building remained unexcavated.

## What archaeologists found

The excavation exposed a multiroom building with floors and burned debris, along with ceramic vessels and censers, a greenstone plaque, a ceramic flute and obsidian artifacts. Its function remains unknown.

## Research history
