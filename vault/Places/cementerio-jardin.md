# Cementerio Jardín

## Description

A salvage-excavated locality about 700 m southeast of the main Tazumal group. The excavation exposed a multiroom building with floors and burned debris, along with ceramic vessels and censers, a greenstone plaque, a ceramic flute and obsidian artifacts. [[Papers/earliest_pipil#Page 25|Source: PDF pp. 25–27 (printed pp. 23–25), ‘The Cementerio Jardín Locality’]]

## Studies

### 2009: Reinterpretation

Haberland's dated author note explicitly reconsidered the earlier Toltec interpretation. [[Papers/earliest_pipil#Page 2|Source: earliest_pipil.pdf, PDF p. 2]]

### 1988: Archival synthesis

Haberland synthesized and presented the excavation results. [[Papers/earliest_pipil#Page 2|Source: earliest_pipil.pdf, PDF pp. 2, 25–33]]

### 1985: Fieldwork

Haberland conducted the salvage excavation; the PDF corrects the field year to 1985. In 1985, a salvage project used test-unit transects and then exposed 80 m² around stone-and-adobe remains. [[Papers/earliest_pipil#Page 18|Source: earliest_pipil.pdf, PDF p. 18]]
