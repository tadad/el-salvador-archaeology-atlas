# San Jacinto church east findspot

## Description

A reported findspot east of the church of San Jacinto where Joaquín Hernández told Lardé that excavations had exposed several Indigenous utensils. Lardé did not state their depth or stratigraphic context, so this locality cannot be assumed to belong to the same buried horizon as his nearby road-cut observation. It is also distinct from the later [[Places/cerro-zapote-san-jacinto|Cerro Zapote]] excavation. [[Papers/arqueologia-cuzcatleca-valle-de-san-salvador#Page 12|Source: PDF p. 12]]

## Studies

### 1924: Published secondhand report

Lardé recorded Hernández's report but supplied no excavation date, artifact description, stratigraphy, map, or coordinate. The record is retained as a research lead rather than a confirmed archaeological site.
