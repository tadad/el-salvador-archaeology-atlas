# Cheribon

## Description

The wreck of the steamship Cheribon at Punta Remedios in the Los Cóbanos protected area. Built in 1882 and wrecked at Punta Remedios on 12 April 1902. The 2021 dives identified dispersed hull elements, machinery spaces, superstructure elements, and anchoring and mooring equipment. [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 201|Source: PDF pp. 201–203]]

## Studies

### February–March 2021: Field investigation

Two visits and three dives documented surviving vessel structure and machinery; sediment and currents limited visibility. The 2021 dives identified dispersed hull elements, machinery spaces, superstructure elements, and anchoring and mooring equipment. [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 203|Source: identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 203]]

### 2015: Protection-area delimitation

A project delimited a protection area for the Douglas and Cheribon wrecks. [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 202|Source: identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 202–203]]

### 2005: Site registration

The first registration used the local name Chirigon and provided a brief description. The wreck was first registered in 2005. [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 202|Source: identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 202]]
