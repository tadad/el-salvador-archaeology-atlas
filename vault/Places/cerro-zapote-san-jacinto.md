# Cerro Zapote, San Jacinto

## Overview

An excavated locality on Cerro Zapote where archaeological material occurred both in buried humus and in the volcanic ash above it.

## Chronology

The lower deposit was later assigned to the Middle–Late Formative; a 1975 synthesis also lists Cerro El Zapote among Postclassic sites, but the mixed upper deposit is not securely dated.

## How it was investigated

In January 1926, investigators excavated beside a road across the hill above the Río Acelhuate, opposite Finca Modelo. The report gives no coordinate or trench dimensions.

## What archaeologists found

The excavation recovered a pottery head, numerous sherds, and fragments of obsidian blades. The upper ash also yielded a mixed assemblage that the report could not separate securely.

## Research history

### January 1926 — Fieldwork

A stratigraphic excavation tested lower- and upper-volcanic-ash assemblages.

### lothrop_pottery_types.pdf

**Citation:** PDF p. 19 (printed p. 175), lower-deposit pottery and figurine

[[Papers/lothrop_pottery_types#Page 19|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://fundar.org.sv/referencias/lothrop_pottery_types.pdf#page=19)

The primary report describes a modeled pottery head, a figurine recovered by Jorge Lardé from the same lower layer, and thick-walled sherds with orange wash, incised lines or grooves, and parallel-line painted decoration. Its period and culture terminology is historical and should not be treated as a modern cultural attribution.

### lothrop_pottery_types.pdf

**Citation:** PDF p. 29 (printed p. 185), mixed upper-ash assemblage

[[Papers/lothrop_pottery_types#Page 29|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://fundar.org.sv/referencias/lothrop_pottery_types.pdf#page=29)

Lothrop records figurines, plumbate or glazed ware, and vessels with Tlaloc heads in the upper ash, while warning that the upper materials could not be separated with the methods then used. The page does not establish a single date or culture for that mixed deposit.

### casasola_panorama.pdf

**Citation:** PDF p. 4 (printed p. 719), central-region chronology

[[Papers/casasola_panorama#Page 4|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://fundar.org.sv/referencias/casasola_panorama.pdf#page=4)

Casasola assigns the Cerro Zapote lower finds to the Middle–Late Formative and separately lists Cerro El Zapote among central-region Postclassic sites. The synthesis does not securely date the mixed upper deposit or assign the full site to one culture.
