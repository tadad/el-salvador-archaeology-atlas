# San Ramón (11-4)

## Description

Two 2025 resolutions from the [[Organizations/ministerio-de-cultura|Ministry of Culture]] identify San Ramón as registered archaeological site 11-4, but neither describes the object, deposit, or feature that led to its registration. No public source located for this record supplies a period, site type, artifact list, or original field report. [Resolution DA-127-2025, PDF pp. 50–51](https://www.transparencia.gob.sv/descarga_archivo.php?id=NjI5ODQ3) [Resolution DA-136-2025, PDF pp. 74–75](https://www.transparencia.gob.sv/descarga_archivo.php?id=NjI5ODQ3)

## Nearby evidence is not site evidence

One resolution reports that a nearby development parcel had no archaeological material or features. The other records sparse, dispersed ceramic fragments on a different parcel, but explicitly says that their scarcity and lack of context prevent associating them with San Ramón or another nearby registered site. The atlas therefore does not attribute those fragments to San Ramón.

## Location and uncertainty

The resolutions give two relative locations from separate development parcels. The marker is a generalized best-fit point in the resulting Izalco–Cuntán–Cangrejera corridor. Because neither parcel boundary nor the registry coordinate is public, it should not be used for navigation, land identification, or site-boundary analysis.
