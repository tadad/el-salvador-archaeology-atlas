# Texistepeque Group II

## Description

A reported mound group about 2 km south of Texistepeque, east of the highway to Metapán. Undated; the report gives no occupation date or cultural assignment. Five or six mounds were reported. [[Papers/longyear#Page 16|Source: PDF pp. 16, 29 (printed pp. 9, 22), Part II survey scope, Texistepeque Group II entry, and fig. 15]]

## Studies

### 1940–1942: Fieldwork

Boggs mapped the southern mound group during the western reconnaissance; the exact visit year is unstated. The western survey visited and described the group sometime in 1940–1942, but gives no site-specific visit date or field methods. Five or six mounds were reported. Mounds 1 and 2 were about 5.5–6 m high; a small house stood on Mound 1, but the rest of the site appeared undisturbed.
