# Aguachapio burial record

## Description

A registration card lists Aguachapio in Jujutla and reports at least three low mounds and a possible burial. The card assigns the locality to the Late Preclassic and is dated February 24, 1986. Reported finds include pottery, a plano-convex handstone and obsidian. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 47|Source: PDF pp. 47–49 (printed pp. 47–49), registration-card caveat and Table 1]]

## Studies

### 2016: Archival synthesis

A national burial review reassessed the archaeology-department registration card and its evidentiary limits. The publication reproduces a registration-card summary; it does not document controlled excavation. The publication warns that these card records are not independently verified. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 47|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 47–49]]

### 24 February 1986: Site recording

Paul Amaroli registered the site and its reported burial context. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 48|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 48–49]]
