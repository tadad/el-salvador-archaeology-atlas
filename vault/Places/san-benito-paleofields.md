# Colonia San Benito paleofields

## Description

Road construction exposed agricultural ridges beneath presumed Tierra Blanca Joven ash: 45 furrows in a north cut and 66 in a south cut. [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 119|Source: PDF p. 119–120]]
