# El Zapote (Cooperativa San Isidro)

## Description

An excavated site on a small flat area of El Zapote mountain within Cooperativa San Isidro. Three elongated mounds enclose a small plaza on a leveled terrace. Postclassic, but the report could not determine whether the occupation was early or late within that period. Excavation documented earthen-and-stone construction and fill used to level the terrace. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 44|Source: PDF p. 44 (printed p. 44), introduction, site description, and Figure 1]]

## Studies

### 2013: Fieldwork

A small excavation season included mapping, three test pits, and ceramic comparison. Investigators recorded 896 total-station points and opened three test pits across the plaza and the bases of two mounds. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 43|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 43–56]]

### 2011: Site recording

The site was registered and revisited before excavation. Excavation documented earthen-and-stone construction and fill used to level the terrace. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 44|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF p. 44]]
