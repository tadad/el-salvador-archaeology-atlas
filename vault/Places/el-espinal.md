# El Espinal

## Description

A mapped architectural site east of the Lempa within the wider San Lorenzo project landscape. It remained outside the reservoir’s flooded zone. The principal evidence is the surviving arrangement of mounds and platforms. [[Papers/2016-barrera-puntas-flecha-oriente#Page 102|Source: PDF p. 102 (printed p. 87), Figure 4: San Lorenzo sites outside the inundation zone]] [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 38|Source: PDF p. 38 (printed p. 27), site description and photographs]]
