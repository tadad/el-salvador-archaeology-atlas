# El Chaparral

## Description

An excavated locality on a steep part of the Torola River's south bank. It has retaining walls on three terraces, but their exact date is unknown. The walls postdate Late Classic material beneath them and may be colonial. Excavators documented poorly preserved walls and a semicircular feature. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 61|Source: PDF p. 61 (printed p. 61), abstract and site setting]] [[Papers/2016-barrera-puntas-flecha-oriente#Page 54|Source: PDF pp. 54–56, 79–91, site summary and three-point analysis]]

## Studies

### 2016: Collection analysis

A projectile-point thesis analyzed three artifacts from El Chaparral. [[Papers/2016-barrera-puntas-flecha-oriente#Page 54|Source: Análisis morfológico y funcional de las puntas de flecha del oriente de El Salvador, PDF pp. 54–56, 79–91]]

### 16 January–15 February 2013: Fieldwork

A rescue excavation opened 12 test pits. Twelve test pits, mostly 2 × 2 metres, sampled the roughly 100 × 50 metre locality and its three terraces. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 61|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 61, 65–67]]

### 2008–2009: Site recording

The systematic regional survey discovered and recorded the site. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 61|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 61, 65–67]]
