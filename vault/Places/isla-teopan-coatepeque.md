# Isla Teopán, Lake Coatepeque

## Description

Construction near the shore of Isla Teopán exposed an archaeological deposit and a monumental potbelly sculpture in early 1996. The report lists the sculpture, four mushroom stones, Bolinas figurines, and ceramics. [[Papers/teopan#Page 1|Source: PDF pp. 1–2 (printed pp. 51–52), Location and Discovery, figs. 1–3, and preliminary dating]]

## Studies

### Early 1996: Fieldwork

Construction exposed archaeological deposits, and Amaroli inspected the exposure and surface sherds. An inspection identified Chul- and Caynac-complex sherds on the surface after construction exposed the deposit. The article documents no controlled excavation and gives only limited context for the sculpture. The report lists the sculpture, four mushroom stones, Bolinas figurines, and ceramics. Amaroli identified the sculpture as a woman and proposed that potbelly sculptures represented pregnant women. [[Papers/teopan#Page 1|Source: teopan.pdf, PDF p. 1]]
