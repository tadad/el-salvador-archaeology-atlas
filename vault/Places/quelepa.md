# Quelepa

## Description

An excavated archaeological center about 8 kilometres northwest of San Miguel. A 1973 account describes a ceremonial center covering more than half a square kilometre. The excavated ceramics and architecture were used to establish an archaeological sequence for eastern El Salvador. [[Papers/wyllys_flautas#Page 13|Source: PDF p. 13 (printed p. 7), site and excavation summary]]

## Studies

### 2017: Site recording

The ballcourt was photographically and aerially documented for a new architectural interpretation. [Source: García Hernández thesis, PDF pp. 163–170](https://biblioteca.utec.edu.sv/siab/virtual/tesis/941001110.pdf#page=163)

### 2016: Collection analysis

A projectile-point thesis analyzed 13 artifacts from Quelepa. [[Papers/2016-barrera-puntas-flecha-oriente#Page 48|Source: Análisis morfológico y funcional de las puntas de flecha del oriente de El Salvador, PDF pp. 48–49, 79–91]]

### 2006–2008: Condition assessment

The Atlas team inspected the site, interviewed custodians, and documented exposed and damaged architecture. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 35|Source: Atlas Arqueológico de la Región de Oriente de El Salvador, PDF p. 35]]

### By 1973: Artifact study

Boggs documented wheeled figurines and related artifacts reported from near Quelepa, including material from a field south of the western group. Some contexts depended on collector reports, so their associations are not uniformly secure. [[Papers/ruedas#Page 6|Source: ruedas.pdf, PDF pp. 6–16]]

### 1967–1969: Fieldwork

Andrews conducted the major excavation project at Quelepa. The excavated ceramics and architecture were used to establish an archaeological sequence for eastern El Salvador. [[Papers/wyllys_flautas#Page 13|Source: wyllys_flautas.pdf, PDF p. 13]]
