# Chinameca burial

## Description

Entierro 1 de Chinameca is an individual burial found fortuitously; its exact findspot and discovery date are not published. The associated mini-incensario suggests either the Terminal Late Classic (AD 700–900) or Early Postclassic (AD 900–1200). The burial included a mini-incensario with a serpent-shaped zoomorphic handle. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|Source: PDF pp. 60, 68–71 (printed pp. 60, 68–71)]]

## Studies

### 2015: Collection analysis

Chinameca Burial 1 was selected for osteological and paleopathological analysis. A 2015 paleopathology project examined the remains held by the Department of Archaeology; the report does not document a controlled excavation. The burial had already been disturbed by its finders and exposed to rain for two days. The burial included a mini-incensario with a serpent-shaped zoomorphic handle. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 60, 68–71]]

### Undated: Site recording

The burial was fortuitously discovered and disturbed; the surviving source gives no discovery or recovery year. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 68|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 68–71]]
