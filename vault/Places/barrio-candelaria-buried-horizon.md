# Barrio Candelaria buried horizon

## Description

A findspot in Barrio Candelaria where Lardé collected fragments of Indigenous utensils from dark clay soil beneath the white volcanic deposit. He described the exposure as a cut made to extend the street passing between the cathedral and university, but did not publish enough information to reconstruct its modern position. It represents one observation within a regional buried horizon rather than a securely bounded archaeological site. [[Papers/arqueologia-cuzcatleca-valle-de-san-salvador#Page 12|Source: PDF p. 12]]

## Studies

### 1924: Published observation

Lardé reported personally removing artifact fragments from the exposed buried soil. The account supplies a stratigraphic relationship but no controlled excavation, artifact catalogue, map, or coordinate.
