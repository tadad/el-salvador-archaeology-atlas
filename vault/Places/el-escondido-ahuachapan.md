# El Escondido

## Overview

A named archaeological locality newly registered during the 2007 southern Ahuachapán survey.

## Chronology

Preclassic survey context; recorded in 2007.

## How it was investigated

Pedestrian reconnaissance located and registered surface material.

## What archaeologists found

The occurrence helped demonstrate that early settlement was distributed across the coastal plain rather than concentrated only at Cara Sucia and Atalaya.

## Research history

### 2007 — Site recording

The southern Ahuachapán reconnaissance newly registered El Escondido.

**Evidence:** atalaya-acajutla.pdf, article p. 143
