# Joya de Cerén

## Description

A Middle/Late Classic village buried by a volcanic eruption, where excavations exposed structures and cultivated fields. The park history dates the burial to about 640 CE. GPR recognized eight of nine excavated structures within the survey grids and 18 additional structure-like anomalies awaiting confirmation. [[Papers/parks#Page 2|Source: PDF p. 2, Joya de Cerén park history]] [[Papers/1995-conyers-gpr-ceren#Page 18|Source: PDF pp. 18–23, structure identification and paleolandscape interpretation]]

## Studies

### 2014: Reinterpretation

A site study reassessed identity, agency, representation, and site-management narratives. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 236|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF pp. 236–257]]

### 2013: Collection analysis

A thesis analyzed Copador vessel contexts, forms, and chronology. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 10|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 10–39]]

### 1989–2011: Fieldwork

Repeated excavation seasons investigated architecture, households, daily life, and crops; the project was ongoing when reported at the 2011 symposium. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 5|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 5, 9 (printed pp. 431, 435)]]

### 1994: Fieldwork

A digital GPR campaign added 3,800 m of 300 and 500 MHz profiles; combined with digitized 1979 data, five grids covered about 2 ha. Investigators collected more than 7.8 km of GPR profiles across five grids, converted travel time to depth, and modeled the buried living surface. [[Papers/1995-conyers-gpr-ceren#Page 8|Source: 1995-conyers-gpr-ceren.pdf, PDF pp. 8–9]]

### August 1992: Fieldwork

A rainy-season GPR attempt suffered signal attenuation at 2–3 m and identified no significant anomalies. [[Papers/1995-conyers-gpr-ceren#Page 7|Source: 1995-conyers-gpr-ceren.pdf, PDF p. 7]]

### 1979–1980: Fieldwork

The initial project conducted several geophysical investigations, including magnetic-resistance and radar methods. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 5|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 5 (printed p. 431)]]

### June 1979: Fieldwork

A dry-season 80 MHz GPR survey collected roughly 4,000 m of profiles over about 1 ha; drilling later confirmed one structure-like anomaly. [[Papers/1995-conyers-gpr-ceren#Page 6|Source: 1995-conyers-gpr-ceren.pdf, PDF pp. 6–8]]
