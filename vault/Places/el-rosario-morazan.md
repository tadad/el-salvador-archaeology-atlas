# El Rosario (Morazán)

## Description

An obsidian find locality on the plaza of El Rosario village. The report lists 63 obsidian pieces, mostly small chips. [[Papers/haberland_morazan#Page 1|Source: PDF p. 1, pp. 3–4, and p. 7 (printed pp. 119, 121–122, and 125), reconnaissance dates, Figures 1–2, and El Rosario account]]

## Studies

### 29 June–2 July 1954: Fieldwork

Haberland visited the locality during the Morazán reconnaissance and recorded archaeological material. Obsidian was collected from the surface. No controlled excavation is documented. The report lists 63 obsidian pieces, mostly small chips. Three were worked tools or fragments: two unifacial points or point fragments and one bifacial point-or-knife tip; a quartz chip was also found.
