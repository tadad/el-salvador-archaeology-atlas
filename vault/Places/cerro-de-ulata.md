# Cerro de Ulata

## Description

Cerro de Ulata is a surveyed mound settlement in Teotepeque, divided into eastern and western groups along two north–south ridge axes. The survey recorded at least 25 mounds: at least 11 in the eastern group and 14 in the western group, with several small plazas. [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 89|Source: PDF pp. 89, 99, 109–110 (printed pp. 89, 99, 109–110), abstract, fig. 1, Cerro de Ulata entry, and route model]]

## Studies

### 2022: Reinterpretation

A GIS/LiDAR study geospatially reanalyzed the site; this is the study's completion/submission year, not a field visit. A later study modeled routes and visibility; no controlled excavation is documented. The survey recorded at least 25 mounds: at least 11 in the eastern group and 14 in the western group, with several small plazas.
