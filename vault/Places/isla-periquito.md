# Isla Periquito midden

## Description

A pre-Hispanic shell midden reported on Isla Periquito in the Gulf of Fonseca. A 1983 map labels El Periquito as Classic, but it does not explain the dating evidence. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 44|Source: PDF p. 44 (printed p. 44), ‘Antecedentes de concheros prehispánicos en El Salvador’]] [[Papers/boggs_cayuco#Page 1|Source: PDF p. 1 (printed p. 205), map of coastal archaeological sites]]

## Studies

### 1978: Fieldwork

Andrews's Pacific-coast survey reported Isla Periquito. A published synthesis links the record to a 1978 Pacific-coast survey but gives no site-specific method. No excavation is documented. Only a pre-Hispanic shell midden is reported. The sources do not describe its contents, size, associated artifacts, or function. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 44|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF p. 44]]
