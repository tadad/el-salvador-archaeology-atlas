# Isla Periquito midden

## Overview

A pre-Hispanic shell midden reported on Isla Periquito in the Gulf of Fonseca.

## Chronology

A 1983 map labels El Periquito as Classic, but it does not explain the dating evidence.

## How it was investigated

A published synthesis links the record to a 1978 Pacific-coast survey but gives no site-specific method. No excavation is documented.

## What archaeologists found

Only a pre-Hispanic shell midden is reported. The sources do not describe its contents, size, associated artifacts, or function.

## Research history

### 1978 — Fieldwork

Andrews's Pacific-coast survey reported Isla Periquito.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-53#Page 44|anales-53.pdf, PDF p. 44]]

## Location and uncertainty

The marker identifies the island, not the shell midden’s position or footprint.

## Sources

### anales-53.pdf

**Citation:** PDF p. 44 (printed p. 44), ‘Antecedentes de concheros prehispánicos en El Salvador’

[[Papers/anales-53#Page 44|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/download/revista-anales-53/?wpdmdl=3359#page=44)

The synthesis says Anthony P. Andrews surveyed El Salvador’s Pacific coast in 1978 in search of pre-Hispanic salt-production centers and mentions a pre-Hispanic shell midden on Isla Periquito. It does not publish a site coordinate, describe the midden’s contents or extent, or document excavation.

### boggs_cayuco.pdf

**Citation:** PDF p. 1 (printed p. 205), map of coastal archaeological sites

[[Papers/boggs_cayuco#Page 1|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://fundar.org.sv/referencias/boggs_cayuco.pdf#page=1)

Boggs’s map plots El Periquito as a Classic-period coastal archaeological site. The small-scale map supports the broad location and period assignment but provides no site-specific coordinate, dating evidence, field method, or description of finds.
