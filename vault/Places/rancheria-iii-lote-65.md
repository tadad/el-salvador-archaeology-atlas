# Ranchería III / Lote 65

## Description

Four test operations in Lote 65, Cantón Joya de Cerén, recovered a small quantity of ceramic material. The investigator proposed a Postclassic date from the ceramics' form and color together with their stratigraphic position. The published synthesis gives no sherd count, diagnostic ware names, structures, burials, lithics, or other features, so those details remain unknown. [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 69|Source: PDF p. 69]]

## Distinction from Ranchería I and II

The synthesis documents [[Places/rancheria-i-ii|Ranchería I and II]] as two parcels investigated in September–October 1997, while its bibliography identifies the separate February 1999 Lote 65 work as “Lotificación La Ranchería III.” The differing lot, field season, report, and intervention support a separate atlas record, although the missing parcel map prevents a precise dot.
