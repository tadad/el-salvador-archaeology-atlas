# Peñate

## Description

An architectural group within greater Chalchuapa where thirteen elongated structures and one circular structure were recorded. C1-8 had two stone-and-earth terraces with vertical slab walls; C1-9 had at least one such terrace. [[Papers/chalchuapa-architecture#Page 5|Source: PDF pp. 5–7, printed pp. 204–206]]

## Studies

### 2006: Architectural synthesis

Shibata summarized Peñate’s structures, construction, and Postclassic assignments from the earlier Chalchuapa project. [[Papers/chalchuapa-architecture#Page 5|Source: PDF pp. 5–7, printed pp. 204–206]]

### By 1970: Partial excavation

Structures C1-8 and C1-9 had been partly excavated during the earlier Chalchuapa research, but the synthesis does not give a Peñate-specific season.
