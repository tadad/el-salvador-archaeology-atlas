# Peñate

## Overview

A Postclassic neighborhood within greater Chalchuapa, composed of thirteen elongated platforms and one circular structure.

## Chronology

Most strongly associated with the Postclassic, particularly the later occupation of Chalchuapa; investigated in 1969.

## How it was investigated

Sharer’s project mapped the platform group and partially excavated Structures C1-8 and C1-9.

## What archaeologists found

The excavated platforms had two stone-and-earth terraces with vertical slab facings, showing substantial architecture outside the protected monumental parks.

## Research history

### 1968–1970 — Fieldwork

The Sharer Chalchuapa project partially excavated Peñate; no site-specific season within the range is stated.
