# Peñate

## Overview

An architectural group within greater Chalchuapa where thirteen elongated structures and one circular structure were recorded.

## Chronology

Partially excavated Structure C1-8 was assigned to the Postclassic and C1-9 to the Late Postclassic.

## How it was investigated

The published synthesis reports that Structures C1-8 and C1-9 had been partially excavated. It does not identify the exact field season or excavation units.

## What archaeologists found

C1-8 had two stone-and-earth terraces with vertical slab walls; C1-9 had at least one such terrace.

## Research history

### 2006 — Architectural synthesis

Shibata summarized Peñate’s structures, construction, and Postclassic assignments from the earlier Chalchuapa project.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/chalchuapa-architecture#Page 5|PDF pp. 5–7, printed pp. 204–206]]

### By 1970 — Partial excavation

Structures C1-8 and C1-9 had been partly excavated during the earlier Chalchuapa research, but the synthesis does not give a Peñate-specific season.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/chalchuapa-architecture#Page 2|PDF p. 2, printed p. 201]]; [[Papers/chalchuapa-architecture#Page 5|PDF pp. 5–6, printed pp. 204–205]]
