# San Benito, southern Ahuachapán survey

## Overview

One of the named archaeological localities revisited or registered during the 2007 survey of southern Ahuachapán’s coastal plain.

## Chronology

Preclassic material was the survey’s principal concern; visited in 2007.

## How it was investigated

Pedestrian reconnaissance and GPS recording placed the site within a network of early coastal settlements.

## What archaeologists found

Surface artifacts contributed to the comparison of Atalaya with settlements extending west toward the Guatemalan frontier; the cited summary gives no excavation inventory.

## Research history

### 2007 — Site recording

The southern Ahuachapán reconnaissance newly registered San Benito.

**Evidence:** atalaya-acajutla.pdf, article p. 143

## Location and uncertainty

The locality is known, but the 2007 archaeological survey did not publish a site centroid.
