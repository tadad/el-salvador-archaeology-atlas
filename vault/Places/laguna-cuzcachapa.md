# Laguna Cuzcachapa

## Overview

An archaeological site in the Chalchuapa zone known for a long ceramic sequence.

## Chronology

The published summary places the sequence from the Early Formative through the Early Classic.

## How it was investigated

The Pennsylvania project conducted surface collection and test pits in 1968 and excavation in 1969. The local summaries do not describe the site's excavation units or features.

## What archaeologists found

The cited summary identifies a long ceramic sequence. It does not describe architecture, lakeshore deposits or ritual activity at the site.

## Research history

### 2020 — Historical synthesis

A review of Chalchuapa fieldwork identifies 1968 surface collection and test pits followed by excavation at Laguna Cuzcachapa in 1969.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 2009 — Collection analysis

The bibliography records a bachelor's dissertation on Preclassic figurines from Casa Blanca and Laguna Cuzcachapa. The dissertation itself is not in the local corpus.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 16|2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 16]]

### 1969 — Fieldwork

The Pennsylvania project excavated at Laguna Cuzcachapa during a five-month season that also included El Trapiche, Casa Blanca and Las Victorias.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 1968 — Fieldwork

The project conducted surface collection and test pits before the following year's excavations. The summary does not report site-specific results.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]
