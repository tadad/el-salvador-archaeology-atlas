# Laguna Cuzcachapa

## Description

An archaeological site in the Chalchuapa zone known for a long ceramic sequence. The published summary places the sequence from the Early Formative through the Early Classic. The cited summary identifies a long ceramic sequence. [[Papers/casasola_panorama#Page 3|Source: PDF p. 3 (printed p. 717), western-area chronology]]

## Studies

### 2020: Historical synthesis

A review of Chalchuapa fieldwork identifies 1968 surface collection and test pits followed by excavation at Laguna Cuzcachapa in 1969. [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|Source: 2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 2009: Collection analysis

The bibliography records a bachelor's dissertation on Preclassic figurines from Casa Blanca and Laguna Cuzcachapa. The dissertation itself is not in the local corpus. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 16|Source: 2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 16]]

### 1969: Fieldwork

The Pennsylvania project excavated at Laguna Cuzcachapa during a five-month season that also included El Trapiche, Casa Blanca and Las Victorias. [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|Source: 2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 1968: Fieldwork

The project conducted surface collection and test pits before the following year's excavations. The summary does not report site-specific results.
