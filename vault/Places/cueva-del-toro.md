# Cueva del Toro

## Description

A painted rock shelter on the west side of the same ignimbrite massif as Gruta del Espíritu Santo. The 1996 study described about ten or fewer paintings, almost all human figures. Undated. The author tentatively compared the paintings with those at Gruta del Espíritu Santo but did not establish their period. The paintings use shades of red ochre. [[Papers/1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente#Page 4|Source: PDF p. 4 (printed p. 663), ‘El abrigo rocoso del Toro’]]

## Studies

### April 1996: Site recording

A ten-day eastern rock-art project documented and photographed the paintings, traced the four most visible examples, and collected two flint flakes from the surface; no excavation is documented. The paintings use shades of red ochre. Nearly all depict human figures, several with headdresses; one may combine human and bird features. [[Papers/1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente#Page 1|Source: 1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 4–5]]
