# Zapotitán Valley (Bolinas-type figurine finds)

## Description

A broad regional lead for Bolinas-type figurine fragments reported somewhere in the Zapotitán Valley; it is not a single archaeological site. The Bolinas tradition is broadly Middle and Late Preclassic. The article provides no specimen-level date for the valley finds. Fragmentary Bolinas-type figurines are reported primarily from surface, structural-fill, mixed, or secondary deposits. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 3|Source: PDF p. 3 (printed p. 782), regional history of Bolinas-type figurine finds]]
