# La Laguneta

## Overview

A roughly ten-hectare settlement beside the Río Gaspar near the Lempa, with about 15 structures, 13 additional architectural features, an I-shaped ballcourt, plaza, and acropolis.

## Chronology

Continuous occupation from the Late Preclassic through the Late/Terminal Classic. Although one sentence extends the sequence into the Early Postclassic, the same report says the site was abandoned before about 900 CE and its diagnostic table ends with the Lepa phase; the conservative ontology therefore stops at the Classic.

## How it was investigated

The Atlas team mapped the site with total station, GPS, and GIS, excavated seven stratigraphic test pits, exposed part of the ballcourt, and conducted surface collection.

## What archaeologists found

The project documented about 15 structures and 13 additional features, including the ballcourt, acropolis, plaza, residential mounds, and perimeter walls. It recovered 1,883 ceramic sherds and 225 lithic fragments and used the ceramics to construct a Late Preclassic–Late/Terminal Classic sequence.

## Research history

### 2015 — Regional isotopic proxy study

Researchers could not map La Laguneta directly and instead used animal samples from Puente Titihuapa as a regional geological proxy.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-54#Page 122|anales-54.pdf, PDF p. 122]]

### 2006–2008 — Mapping and test excavation

The Atlas project produced a new instrument map, excavated seven test pits, exposed part of the ballcourt, collected surface material, and analyzed the ceramics; the exact field season is unstated.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 45|Atlas Arqueológico de la Región de Oriente de El Salvador, PDF pp. 45–48]]

### By 2010 — Prior archaeological study

A 2014 secondary account cites an earlier study by Amador and colleagues, reporting at least 15 structures, recovered materials, and a Late-to-Terminal Classic occupation. The underlying report is not mapped to a local PDF in this corpus.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-53#Page 81|anales-53.pdf, PDF p. 81]]

## Location and uncertainty

The archaeological report places the site beside the Río Gaspar in Usulután but deliberately withholds site coordinates. The atlas marker therefore retains Puente Titihuapa, the substitute sampling locality selected as a regional geological proxy in a 2015 study; it is not the archaeological site's published location.

- **Coordinates:** 13.6625, -88.4759
- **Placed from:** Puente Titihuapa geological proxy for the broader region
- **Precision:** Approximate proxy; not a site coordinate

[[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 45|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://www.famsi.org/reports/07070es/07070esAmador01.pdf#page=45)

The report identifies La Laguneta among the newly mapped sites and describes total-station, GPS, and GIS methods. It documents a roughly ten-hectare site with about 15 structures and 13 other features, seven test pits, ballcourt exposure, and the ceramic and lithic totals. Its detailed sequence and abandonment discussion support Preclassic and Classic links but not a secure Postclassic link. The site plan has no public geodetic coordinate.
