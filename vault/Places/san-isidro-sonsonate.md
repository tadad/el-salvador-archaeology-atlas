# San Isidro (Sonsonate)

## Description

A large Middle-to-Late Preclassic monumental center in Sonsonate. Drone photogrammetry mapped at least 51 visible structures across more than 6 square kilometres; its largest visible mound, Cerrito 1, faces Trapiche 3 across a plaza. Five complete Bolinas-type figures occupied just over 0.6 m²: three approximately 30 cm cream-paste figures with string-adjustable heads and two smaller orange-brown figures about 18 and 10 cm tall. [Source: PDF pp. 9–10, archaeological verification and conclusions](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jan-Szymanski/publication/363318851_Regional_patterns_local_techniques_Remote_sensing_and_archaeology_at_prehispanic_site_of_San_Isidro_El_Salvador/links/63185bb561e4553b956ec0bc/Regional-patterns-local-techniques-Remote-sensing-and-archaeology-at-prehispanic-site-of-San-Isidro-El-Salvador.pdf#page=9) [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 4|Source: PDF pp. 4–6 (printed pp. 783–785), Cerrito 1 excavation and figurine tableau]]

## Studies

### 2024: Fieldwork

Investigators documented an earlier offering on the floor of Cerrito 1's predecessor structure and incorporated it into the site's depositional interpretation. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 13|Source: 2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 13]]

### 2022: Fieldwork

Excavation near the top of Cerrito 1 exposed a complete Bolinas figurine tableau in a primary context less than 0.5 m below the surface. Five complete figures occupied just over 0.6 m²: three approximately 30 cm cream-paste figures with string-adjustable heads and two smaller orange-brown figures about 18 and 10 cm tall. Four represent females; one may be the first complete male of the type and bears facial tattoo or scarification. Other Cerrito 1 fill contained many figurine fragments and a hollow torso into which the smallest tableau figure fits. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 4|Source: 2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 4]]

### 2021: Fieldwork

GPR, magnetometry, electrical-resistivity survey, and targeted excavations tested the presumed center's subsurface and mound-construction signatures. [Source: 2022-szymanski-et-al-san-isidro-remote-sensing.pdf, PDF pp. 5–10](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jan-Szymanski/publication/363318851_Regional_patterns_local_techniques_Remote_sensing_and_archaeology_at_prehispanic_site_of_San_Isidro_El_Salvador/links/63185bb561e4553b956ec0bc/Regional-patterns-local-techniques-Remote-sensing-and-archaeology-at-prehispanic-site-of-San-Isidro-El-Salvador.pdf#page=5)

### 2018–2021: Site recording

Successive drone-photogrammetry campaigns mapped at least 51 visible structures across more than 6 km²; the survey did not use airborne LiDAR and could not resolve dense shade forest reliably. [Source: 2022-szymanski-et-al-san-isidro-remote-sensing.pdf, PDF pp. 3–4](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jan-Szymanski/publication/363318851_Regional_patterns_local_techniques_Remote_sensing_and_archaeology_at_prehispanic_site_of_San_Isidro_El_Salvador/links/63185bb561e4553b956ec0bc/Regional-patterns-local-techniques-Remote-sensing-and-archaeology-at-prehispanic-site-of-San-Isidro-El-Salvador.pdf#page=3)

### 2018: Fieldwork

The first PASI field season documented a plain stela exposed by ploughing on Trapiche 3. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 8|Source: 2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 8]]
