# Antiguo Cuscatlán (Avenida Navas)

## Description

A buried Middle Preclassic site exposed by utility work in Antiguo Cuscatlán. The excavated portion was cautiously interpreted as residential. The ceramic assignment places the occupation around 900–650 BCE, within the Middle Preclassic. Investigators recorded at least seven burials; no artifacts were associated with them. [[Papers/milpas#Page 7|Source: PDF pp. 7–8, ‘La Tefra Cuzcatan’]] [[Papers/antiguo_preclasico#Page 1|Source: PDF pp. 1, 3, introduction and burials]]

## Studies

### 1998: Reinterpretation

The Avenida Navas stratigraphy and ceramics were used to define and date the Cuzcatan tephra and its buried agricultural landscape. The cultural layer also contained pottery, obsidian, burned earth, charcoal, floor segments, and stones interpreted as an earth oven. [[Papers/milpas#Page 1|Source: milpas.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 7–9]]

### 1987: Fieldwork

A rescue investigation documented burials, artifacts, and stratigraphy exposed by a utility trench. Investigators recorded at least seven burials; no artifacts were associated with them. [[Papers/antiguo_preclasico#Page 1|Source: antiguo_preclasico.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 3, 6, 11]]
