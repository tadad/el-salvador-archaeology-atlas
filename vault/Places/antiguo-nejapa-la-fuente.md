# Antiguo Nejapa / La Fuente

## Overview

A surveyed locality recorded as La Fuente and Nejapa Viejo, with pre-Hispanic and colonial surface material. Its identification as the former town of Nejapa is a historical and archaeological interpretation, not the result of excavation.

## Chronology

Surface material collected in the late 1970s was assigned to the Late Classic and Early Postclassic, about 600–1200 CE. The report identifies the locality with colonial Nejapa, whose inhabitants moved after the 1658 El Playón eruption.

## How it was investigated

The Protoclassic Project recorded the site and analyzed surface material in the late 1970s. A probable 1994 revisit registered it as Nejapa Viejo, a 2006 visit tested archaeological geophysics, and PAHES revisited it in 2008; no controlled excavation is documented.

## What archaeologists found

The report describes terraces interpreted as possible property plots and says pre-Hispanic and colonial material could be collected across the property. It does not report the results of the 2006 geophysical tests.
