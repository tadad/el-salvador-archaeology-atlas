# Antiguo Nejapa / La Fuente

## Description

A surveyed locality recorded as La Fuente and Nejapa Viejo, with pre-Hispanic and colonial surface material. Its identification as the former town of Nejapa is a historical and archaeological interpretation, not the result of excavation. Surface material collected in the late 1970s was assigned to the Late Classic and Early Postclassic, about 600–1200 CE. The report identifies the locality with colonial Nejapa, whose inhabitants moved after the 1658 El Playón eruption. The report describes terraces interpreted as possible property plots and says pre-Hispanic and colonial material could be collected across the property. [Source: PDF pp. 7–9 (printed pp. 845–847), Antiguo Nejapa entry and phase summary](https://www.asociaciontikal.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/63.09-Erquicia_09.pdf#page=7)

## Studies

### 2008: Site recording

PAHES revisited and inventoried the locality during its second field phase. [[Papers/2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase#Page 7|Source: 2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase.pdf, PDF pp. 7–9 (printed pp. 845–847)]]

### 2006: Fieldwork

A site visit tested archaeological geophysical sounding methods. It does not report the results of the 2006 geophysical tests. [[Papers/2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase#Page 8|Source: 2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase.pdf, PDF p. 8 (printed p. 846)]]

### Probably 1994: Site recording

Paul Amaroli revisited and registered La Fuente as Nejapa Viejo; the source marks the year as probable. [[Papers/2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase#Page 8|Source: 2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase.pdf, PDF p. 8 (printed p. 846)]]

### Late 1970s: Site recording

The Protoclassic Project registered La Fuente and analyzed collected surface material. [[Papers/2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase#Page 8|Source: 2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase.pdf, PDF p. 8 (printed p. 846)]]
