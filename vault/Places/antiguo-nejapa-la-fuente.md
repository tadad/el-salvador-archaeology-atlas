# Antiguo Nejapa / La Fuente

## Overview

A candidate location for old Nejapa in the Río Sucio landscape, abandoned after the 1658 El Playón eruption disrupted the settlement.

## Chronology

Colonial, especially the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.

## How it was investigated

Archaeologists combined pedestrian survey with geophysical testing to look for buried settlement remains.

## What archaeologists found

Surface and subsurface anomalies support a buried colonial community near the old water source, although the town plan remains incomplete.

## Research history

### 2008 — Site recording

PAHES revisited and inventoried the settlement during its second field phase.

**Evidence:** 2010-erquicia-sitios-historicos-fase-2.pdf, PDF pp. 8–9 (printed pp. 846–847)

### 2006 — Fieldwork

A site visit tested archaeological geophysical sounding methods.

**Evidence:** 2010-erquicia-sitios-historicos-fase-2.pdf, PDF p. 8 (printed p. 846)

### Probably 1994 — Site recording

Paul Amaroli revisited and registered La Fuente as Nejapa Viejo; the source marks the year as probable.

**Evidence:** 2010-erquicia-sitios-historicos-fase-2.pdf, PDF p. 8 (printed p. 846)

### Late 1970s — Site recording

The Protoclassic Project registered La Fuente and analyzed collected surface material.

**Evidence:** 2010-erquicia-sitios-historicos-fase-2.pdf, PDF p. 8 (printed p. 846)
