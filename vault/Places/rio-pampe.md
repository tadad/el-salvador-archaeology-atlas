# Pampe Group

## Description

A heavily damaged archaeological group on the south bank of the Río Pampe, recorded on Finca San Marcos and nearby land. Pre-Hispanic, otherwise undated in the cited sources. The report describes a rectangular terraced base, two plazas with stone walls, adobe-and-scoria construction, and surface pottery sherds. [[Papers/longyear#Page 24|Source: PDF pp. 24–25 (printed pp. 17–18), Pampe Group entry and fig. 6]]
