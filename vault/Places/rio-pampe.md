# Pampe Group

## Overview

A heavily damaged archaeological group on the south bank of the Río Pampe, recorded on Finca San Marcos and nearby land.

## Chronology

Pre-Hispanic, otherwise undated in the cited sources.

## How it was investigated

Longyear documented the group during a 1941–1942 reconnaissance expedition. No Pampe-specific excavation or testing is documented in the cited sources.

## What archaeologists found

The report describes a rectangular terraced base, two plazas with stone walls, adobe-and-scoria construction, and surface pottery sherds.

## Research history

### 1941–1942 — Site recording

The Longyear expedition documented Río Pampe; the exact visit date is not stated.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/longyear#Page 11|longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 11, 24–25]]

### 1920 — Fieldwork

Lardé conducted reconnaissance at Pampe.

**Evidence:** finca-rosita-santa-ana.pdf, PDF p. 27

### longyear.pdf

**Citation:** PDF pp. 24–25 (printed pp. 17–18), Pampe Group entry and fig. 6

[[Papers/longyear#Page 24|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://fundar.org.sv/referencias/longyear.pdf#page=24)

PDF pp. 24–25 (printed pp. 17–18) and fig. 6 place the Pampe Group on Finca San Marcos and nearby land on the south bank of the Río Pampe, along both sides of a cart track. The report describes a heavily damaged rectangular terraced base, two stone-walled plazas, adobe-and-scoria construction, uncertain low hillocks, and surface sherds. It reports no carved stone or complete pottery vessels and provides no controlled excavation, secure occupation date, cultural assignment, geodetic coordinate, or site boundary.
