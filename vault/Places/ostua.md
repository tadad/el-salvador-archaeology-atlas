# Ostúa church ruins

## Description

Ostúa preserves the facade of a colonial church and the base of an atrial cross about 20 m west. The report presents 1733 earthquake damage and flooding between 1734 and 1740 as unresolved explanations for the town's abandonment. The report documents the church facade and atrial-cross base; further wall foundations nearby are described only as probable.
