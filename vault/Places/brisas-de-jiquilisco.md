# Brisas de Jiquilisco

## Overview

A coastal settlement a few kilometres inland from Jiquilisco Bay. Its testing helps connect the bay’s estuarine resources with the communities that occupied the adjacent plain.

## Chronology

Late/Terminal Preclassic and Late Classic–Terminal, based on the ceramic groups tabulated by the Atlas. Investigated during the 2006–2008 project.

## How it was investigated

The team excavated twenty test pits across the site after locating it during regional survey.

## What archaeologists found

The pits established subsurface occupation and produced ceramic evidence for comparison across the Jiquilisco region. The report treats the collective sample, rather than one spectacular object, as the principal result.

## Research history

### 2006–2008 — Fieldwork

The Atlas project conducted surface reconnaissance, opened 20 test pits, and analyzed recovered material; the exact field season is unstated.

The Atlas places the study area a few kilometres northeast of Bahía de Jiquilisco, documents intensive agricultural disturbance and 20 test pits, and says no architecture survived in primary position. Table 4.4 identifies both Late/Terminal Preclassic and Late Classic–Terminal ceramic groups in the Brisas sample, supporting the two broad period links without an ethnic attribution.
