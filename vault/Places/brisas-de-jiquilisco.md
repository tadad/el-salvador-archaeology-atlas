# Brisas de Jiquilisco

## Description

A coastal settlement a few kilometres inland from Jiquilisco Bay. Its testing helps connect the bay’s estuarine resources with the communities that occupied the adjacent plain. Late/Terminal Preclassic and Late Classic–Terminal, based on the ceramic groups tabulated by the Atlas. The pits established subsurface occupation and produced ceramic evidence for comparison across the Jiquilisco region. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 52|Source: PDF p. 52 (printed p. 41), site description and Table 4.4]]

## Studies

### 2006–2008: Fieldwork

The Atlas project conducted surface reconnaissance, opened 20 test pits, and analyzed recovered material; the exact field season is unstated. The team excavated twenty test pits across the site after locating it during regional survey. The pits established subsurface occupation and produced ceramic evidence for comparison across the Jiquilisco region. The report treats the collective sample, rather than one spectacular object, as the principal result. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 52|Source: Atlas Arqueológico de la Región de Oriente de El Salvador, PDF p. 52]]
