# Hacienda San Diego, Lake Güija

## Description

A group of at least ten low mounds lay around the Santa Ana–Metapán highway near Hacienda San Diego. Pre-Hispanic, otherwise undated. Exposed areas showed boulders set in adobe mortar. [[Papers/longyear#Page 11|Source: PDF pp. 11, 29 (printed pp. 1, 22), expedition dates and San Diego entry with fig. 14]]

## Studies

### 1941–1942: Site recording

The Longyear expedition documented San Diego at Lake Güija; earlier digging is mentioned but not dated. Longyear documented and mapped the mound group during his 1941–1942 expedition. Earlier small excavations had cut into Mounds 1 and 10, but the report does not identify who conducted them or how. Exposed areas showed boulders set in adobe mortar. Five complete pottery vessels, several manos, and one carved stone object were reported; one polychrome bowl was apparently associated with a burial. [[Papers/longyear#Page 11|Source: longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 11, 29]]
