# Hacienda San Diego, Lake Güija

## Overview

A group of at least ten low mounds lay around the Santa Ana–Metapán highway near Hacienda San Diego.

## Chronology

Pre-Hispanic, otherwise undated.

## How it was investigated

Longyear documented and mapped the mound group during his 1941–1942 expedition. Earlier small excavations had cut into Mounds 1 and 10, but the report does not identify who conducted them or how.

## What archaeologists found

Exposed areas showed boulders set in adobe mortar. Five complete pottery vessels, several manos, and one carved stone object were reported; one polychrome bowl was apparently associated with a burial.

## Research history

### 1941–1942 — Site recording

The Longyear expedition documented San Diego at Lake Güija; earlier digging is mentioned but not dated.

The text describes low, eroded mounds, exposed boulder-and-adobe construction, five complete pottery vessels, manos, and one carved worked-stone object.
