# El Tanque / Hacienda El Morrito

## Overview

A Classic-period ritual center at Hacienda El Morrito in the north-central Lempa region.

## Chronology

Classic period. A 1976 publication reports the excavation, but the cited sources do not state when fieldwork occurred.

## How it was investigated

A preliminary report title documents excavations of Mound 3. The cited syntheses provide no site-specific methods, boundary, or excavation coordinate.

## What archaeologists found

El Tanque is associated with Copador-related wares, some stuccoed vessels with Maya motifs, and artifacts related to the Copán–Quiriguá area. The cited pages do not give a Mound 3 find list.

## Research history

### 1989 — Reinterpretation

The Cozatol burial association was used in a revised regional phase chronology.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/earliest_pipil#Page 1|earliest_pipil.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 28]]

### 1980 — Reinterpretation

El Tanque polychromes were compared with the Cihuatán ceramic assemblage.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/bruhns#Page 20|bruhns.pdf, PDF p. 20 (printed p. 10)]]

### By 1976 — Fieldwork

Mound 3 at El Tanque, Hacienda El Morrito, was excavated and reported; the exact field year is unstated.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/drowned#Page 30|drowned.pdf, PDF p. 30 (printed p. 28)]]
