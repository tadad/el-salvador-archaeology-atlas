# El Tanque / Hacienda El Morrito

## Description

A Classic-period ritual center at Hacienda El Morrito in the north-central Lempa region. El Tanque is associated with Copador-related wares, some stuccoed vessels with Maya motifs, and artifacts related to the Copán–Quiriguá area. [[Papers/frontier#Page 8|Source: PDF p. 8 (printed p. 292), Classic-period regional discussion]]

## Studies

### 1989: Reinterpretation

The Cozatol burial association was used in a revised regional phase chronology. [[Papers/earliest_pipil#Page 1|Source: earliest_pipil.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 28]]

### 1980: Reinterpretation

El Tanque polychromes were compared with the Cihuatán ceramic assemblage. [[Papers/bruhns#Page 20|Source: bruhns.pdf, PDF p. 20 (printed p. 10)]]

### By 1976: Fieldwork

Mound 3 at El Tanque, Hacienda El Morrito, was excavated and reported; the exact field year is unstated. A preliminary report title documents excavations of Mound 3. The cited pages do not give a Mound 3 find list. [[Papers/drowned#Page 30|Source: drowned.pdf, PDF p. 30 (printed p. 28)]]
