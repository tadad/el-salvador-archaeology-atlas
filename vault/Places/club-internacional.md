# Club Internacional

## Description

A compact deposit of pre-Hispanic objects exposed during basement construction in central San Salvador in 1939. The objects lay within the uppermost volcanic-ash stratum. Boggs tentatively compared the pottery association with late 9th-cycle Maya styles; the deposit itself was not directly dated. The collection included pottery vessels and sherds, a small pottery head, and what Boggs tentatively identified as a peccary tusk. [[Papers/boggs_club#Page 1|Source: PDF pp. 1–2 (printed pp. 238–239), discovery and stratigraphy]]

Boggs described and comparatively analyzed the archaeological material from the contractor find. [[Papers/boggs_club#Page 1|Source: boggs_club.pdf, PDF pp. 1–13]]
