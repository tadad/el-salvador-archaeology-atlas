# Jicalapa

## Description

Jicalapa is a ridge-top archaeological site south of the modern town, with structures divided among three terrace groups. The article discusses Jicalapa within an Early Postclassic (800–1200 CE) regional study, but gives no site-specific dating evidence. The account records 18 structures, including 15 mostly low mounds, as well as some rectangular platform edges and stone alignments.
