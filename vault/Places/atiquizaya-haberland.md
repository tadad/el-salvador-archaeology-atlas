# Atiquizaya locality (Haberland)

## Description

An excavated site near Atiquizaya defined an early ceramic complex for western El Salvador. The published map marks only the town-scale locality, and the excavation parcel is unknown. Haberland tentatively dated the Atiquizaya complex to about 1000–700 BCE, within the Middle Preclassic. Reported ceramic finds include spouts with oval openings, figurines with movable arms, and one decorated bowl-rim sherd.
