# Miramar, Tamanique

## Overview

Miramar is a fourteen-mound archaeological site on a high, narrow part of Loma El Cabro in Tamanique's Cooperativa Acahuaspán.

## Chronology

The cited synthesis includes Miramar in a regional Early Postclassic study (800–1200 CE), but it presents no site-specific dating evidence.

## How it was investigated

The article illustrates Miramar with a LiDAR image, maps it regionally, and models least-cost routes. It does not state when the site was recorded or document excavation.

## What archaeologists found

The source describes mounds 1–2 m high: five apparently form a small plaza at the southeast end, while the others are aligned northwest–southeast along the narrow plateau. It cautiously suggests that some structures may have served as observation points.
