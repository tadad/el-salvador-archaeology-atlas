# Miramar, Tamanique

## Description

Miramar is a fourteen-mound archaeological site on a high, narrow part of Loma El Cabro in Tamanique's Cooperativa Acahuaspán. The cited synthesis includes Miramar in a regional Early Postclassic study (800–1200 CE), but it presents no site-specific dating evidence. The source describes mounds 1–2 m high: five apparently form a small plaza at the southeast end, while the others are aligned northwest–southeast along the narrow plateau.
