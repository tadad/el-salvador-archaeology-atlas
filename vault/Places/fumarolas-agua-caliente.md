# Fumarolas / Agua Caliente

## Description

A roughly 3-hectare archaeological locality on the north bank of the Río Torola. The survey reported no visible structures. The article assigns the site to the Late Classic (600–900 CE), but does not report absolute dating. Surface finds included grinding-stone fragments, manos, a metate, and obsidian flakes. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 70|Source: PDF pp. 70–71 (printed pp. 70–71), ‘Sitio Fumarolas Agua Caliente’]]

## Studies

### 2008–2009: Site recording

A systematic surface survey recorded a medium-density lithic scatter and no pottery. A 2008–2009 dam-impact project conducted systematic surface survey in the reservoir area. The cited sources do not document excavation at this site. Surface finds included grinding-stone fragments, manos, a metate, and obsidian flakes.
