# Fumarolas / Agua Caliente

## Overview

A roughly 3-hectare archaeological locality on the north bank of the Río Torola. The survey reported no visible structures.

## Chronology

The article assigns the site to the Late Classic (600–900 CE), but does not report absolute dating.

## How it was investigated

A 2008–2009 dam-impact project conducted systematic surface survey in the reservoir area. The cited sources do not document excavation at this site.

## What archaeologists found

Surface finds included grinding-stone fragments, manos, a metate, and obsidian flakes. The survey found no pottery.

## Research history

### 2008–2009 — Site recording

A systematic surface survey recorded a medium-density lithic scatter and no pottery.

### anales-53.pdf

**Citation:** PDF pp. 70–71 (printed pp. 70–71), ‘Sitio Fumarolas Agua Caliente’

[[Papers/anales-53#Page 70|Open vault paper]] · [Open cited source](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/download/revista-anales-53/?wpdmdl=3359#page=70)

The site entry places Fumarolas Agua Caliente in Sector E on the Río Torola’s north bank, estimates its area at about 3 hectares, and reports no visible structures. Surface survey found medium-density grinding-stone fragments, including manos and a metate, and obsidian flakes; no pottery was found. The article assigns the site to the Late Classic (600–900 CE) but provides no absolute date. The Carolina entry places that site no more than 200 m northeast of Fumarolas. No site-specific excavation is documented.
