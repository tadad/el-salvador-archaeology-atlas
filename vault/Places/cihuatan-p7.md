# Cihuatán (Structure P-7)

## Description

Structure P-7 is Cihuatán’s principal pyramid, within the city’s western ceremonial center. Limited excavation documented its construction and a burned terminal deposit. For site-wide investigations, see [[Places/cihuatan|Cihuatán]]. Sparse diagnostic pottery is consistent with the Early Postclassic Guazapa phase, about 900–1200 CE. Excavators documented six stepped core terraces, tuff-block facing, a stone platform at the western stair, and partial lava-block paving. [[Papers/P7#Page 4|Source: PDF pp. 4–5 (printed pp. 1–2), summary and introduction]]

## Studies

### 2001–2002: Fieldwork

Researchers made a new topographic survey, excavated stratigraphic units along the pyramid’s western side, and reopened part of the western stair exposed in 1929. [[Papers/P7#Page 4|Source: P7.pdf, PDF pp. 4–5, 12–17]]

### 1929: Fieldwork

Antonio Sol excavated the western stair area later revisited by the P-7 project. [[Papers/P7#Page 12|Source: P7.pdf, PDF pp. 12, 17]]
