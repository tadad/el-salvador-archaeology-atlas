# Paredón de Las Figuras

## Description

A painted volcanic rock wall about 7 km north of Corinto. The published visit was brief, and the report calls for more detailed study. Undated. The report does not assign the paintings to a period. Most paintings are human figures and red; the one identified animal is a red-and-white serpent. [[Papers/1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente#Page 4|Source: PDF p. 4 (printed p. 663), ‘Paredón de Las Figuras’]]

## Studies

### April 1996: Site recording

A ten-day eastern rock-art project documented the shelter and its paintings. During the 1996 regional project, the team made a short description and photographed the paintings; no excavation is documented. Most paintings are human figures and red; the one identified animal is a red-and-white serpent. The paintings are more than 1 m in size and were in poor condition from natural erosion and nearby human activity. [[Papers/1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente#Page 1|Source: 1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 5–6]]
