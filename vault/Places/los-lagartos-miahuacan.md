# Los Lagartos / Miahuacan

## Description

At least nine mounds and several smaller rises were recorded around Río Los Lagartos. Their identification with conquest-era Miahuacan or Mahuaclan is tentative. Undated; the report gives no occupation date or cultural assignment. The two tallest mounds were about 6–7 m high. [[Papers/longyear#Page 16|Source: PDF pp. 16, 30 (printed pp. 9, 23), Part II survey scope, Los Lagartos entry, and fig. 17]]

## Studies

### 1940–1942: Fieldwork

Boggs mapped the mounds and tentatively compared the ruins with historic Miahuacan; the exact visit year is unstated. Boggs’s western survey visited, described, and sketch-mapped Los Lagartos sometime in 1940–1942. No controlled excavation is documented. The two tallest mounds were about 6–7 m high. Long cultivation had turned up much pottery, but no construction was exposed. [[Papers/longyear#Page 16|Source: longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 16, 30 (printed pp. 9, 23)]]
