# Hacienda Santa Bárbara

## Description

A Classic-period ritual center in the north-central Lempa region. The cited sources identify the archaeological place as Hacienda Santa Bárbara; they do not establish La Ciénaga as an alternate name. Santa Bárbara is associated with Copador-related wares, some stuccoed vessels with Maya motifs, and other artifacts related to the Copán–Quiriguá area. [[Papers/frontier#Page 8|Source: PDF p. 8 (printed p. 292), Classic-period regional discussion]] [[Papers/drowned#Page 30|Source: PDF p. 30 (printed p. 28), references]]
