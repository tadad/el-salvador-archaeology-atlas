# Iglesia La Merced, San Salvador

## Description

Archaeologists exhumed the secondary remains attributed to General Manuel José Arce to obtain cultural and osteological samples, recovering sixty-two samples. [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 93|Source: PDF p. 93–94]]
