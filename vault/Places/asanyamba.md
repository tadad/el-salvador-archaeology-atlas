# Asanyamba / Chapernalito

## Description

An approximately eight-acre shell-midden site just off Estero El Chapernalito, also documented as Chapernalito. Commercial extraction and later depredation disturbed the site. Ceramic comparison suggests that most of the salvaged collection is Late Classic; a very small amount may be earlier. The salvaged collection included ceramics, figurines, ear plugs, whistles, chipped and ground stone, shell and worked bone. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 37|Source: PDF pp. 37, 45 (printed pp. 26, 34), condition assessment and field-program summary]] [[Papers/beaudry_asanyamba#Page 1|Source: PDF pp. 2–7, site description, methodology and preliminary ceramic interpretation]]

## Studies

### 2006–2008: Condition assessment and surface collection

The Atlas team revisited the site, mapped its ceremonial core, documented rectangular platforms and heavily damaged shell middens, and collected surface material; the exact visit year is unstated. The salvaged collection included ceramics, figurines, ear plugs, whistles, chipped and ground stone, shell and worked bone. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 37|Source: Atlas Arqueológico de la Región de Oriente de El Salvador, PDF pp. 37, 45]]

### 2004: Photographic condition report

Published photographs identify Chapernalito as Asayamba and document discarded ceramics and disturbed ground. One caption identifies the photographed ceramics as Late Classic; the photographs' exact date is unstated. No controlled excavation is documented. [[Papers/testimony1#Page 4|Source: testimony1.pdf, PDF pp. 4–5]]

### 1982: Collection analysis

A six-week laboratory project sorted approximately sixty sacks of salvaged ceramics, classified a study collection and compared it with regional ceramic sequences. No quantitative analysis was attempted because provenience was limited and collection was nonsystematic. The source warns that limited provenience and nonsystematic collection restrict conclusions about the site. [[Papers/beaudry_asanyamba#Page 4|Source: beaudry_asanyamba.pdf, PDF pp. 4–5]]

### Before 1982, exact year unknown: Salvage

Cultural material was salvaged while commercial extraction removed part of the shell-midden site. The source gives no salvage date and says controlled archaeological investigation had not occurred. [[Papers/beaudry_asanyamba#Page 2|Source: beaudry_asanyamba.pdf, PDF p. 2]]
