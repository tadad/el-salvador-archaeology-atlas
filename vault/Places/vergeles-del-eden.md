# Vergeles del Edén

## Description

An archaeological test at the Vergeles del Edén cemetery identified a pre-Hispanic construction and obsidian-working debris. A later summary treats the structure as contemporary with Early Postclassic remains elsewhere in Chalchuapa, but reports no direct or absolute date from this test. The test exposed a rectilinear construction of stone rows with earth mortar, abundant obsidian debitage, and many projectile points. [[Papers/2007-erquicia-los-gavilanes#Page 3|Source: PDF pp. 3, 13 (printed pp. 856, 866), Vergeles del Edén summary and conclusion]]

## Studies

### 1995: Fieldwork

Fabio Amador carried out an archaeological test at Vergeles del Edén. The available summary does not give its dimensions, location within the cemetery, or stratigraphy. The test exposed a rectilinear construction of stone rows with earth mortar, abundant obsidian debitage, and many projectile points. The original investigator tentatively interpreted the finds as evidence of tool production. [[Papers/2007-erquicia-los-gavilanes#Page 3|Source: 2007-erquicia-los-gavilanes.pdf, PDF p. 3]]
