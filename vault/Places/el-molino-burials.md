# El Molino burial record

## Description

A 1985 registration card reports archaeological material and possible burials in eastern Santa Ana near Cerro Tecana. The card tentatively assigns the material to the Middle Preclassic. The card reports ceramic fragments, figurines, six or eight burials, metates, and manos in a 50 × 50 m area up to 2 m deep. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 47|Source: PDF pp. 47–49, registration-card caveat and table 1, El Molino entry]]

## Studies

### 2016: Archival synthesis

A national burial review reassessed the archaeology-department registration card and its evidentiary limits. The evidence survives as a registration-card entry summarized in a later publication. The card reports ceramic fragments, figurines, six or eight burials, metates, and manos in a 50 × 50 m area up to 2 m deep. The publication warns that card reports are unverified and that reported bones may not have been human. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 47|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 47–49]]

### 11 September 1985: Site recording

Paul Amaroli registered the site and its reported burials and surface material. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 48|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 48–49]]
