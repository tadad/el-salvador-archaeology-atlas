# Texisio

## Overview

A three-mound archaeological site on the upper part of Cerro Texisio, about 281 m above sea level, in Teotepeque.

## Chronology

The cited synthesis includes Texisio among Early Postclassic sites (800–1200 CE), but it presents no site-specific dating evidence.

## How it was investigated

The Costa del Bálsamo project reported discovering the site in 2012. The cited article maps the locality and models least-cost routes; it reports no excavation.

## What archaeologists found

The source describes three mounds forming a small plaza. It provides no artifact inventory.

## Research history

### 2022 — Reinterpretation

A GIS/LiDAR study geospatially reanalyzed the site; this is the study's completion/submission year, not a field visit.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 89|identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 89, 108, 121–125]]

### 2012 — Site recording

The Costa del Bálsamo project discovered and recorded Texisio.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 108|identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 108]]
