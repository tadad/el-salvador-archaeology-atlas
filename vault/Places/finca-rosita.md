# Finca Rosita

## Description

A Preclassic center extending across several modern properties in Santa Ana. Its protected 5.22-manzana nucleus contains six monumental structures around two plazas. The thesis proposes an initial occupation in 1200–900 BCE, greatest activity from 900 BCE to the beginning of the Common Era, and decline during 0–200 CE. The protected area contains six monumental mounds between about one and thirteen metres high. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 48|Source: PDF p. 48, printed p. 40]]

## Studies

### 2005–2006: Ceramic analysis and thesis

Méndez Arévalo analyzed 7,400 sherds between March and September 2005 and submitted the site-specific thesis in 2006. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 48|Source: PDF p. 48, printed p. 40]]; [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 110|Source: PDF pp. 110–111, printed pp. 102–103]]

### 2003: Fieldwork

An investigation on Transportes Pesados property excavated four test pits. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 47|Source: PDF p. 47, printed p. 39]]

### 2001: Fieldwork

Separate projects excavated five test pits on HOTESA property and twenty-five on a Transportes Pesados property. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 47|Source: PDF p. 47, printed p. 39]]

### 1999: Fieldwork

An investigation covered the southern part of Finca Arizona; the thesis gives no field-method details. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 47|Source: PDF p. 47, printed p. 39]]

### 1997–1998: Fieldwork

Projects included inspection, controlled test pits at Finca Rosita and Finca Sinaí, and work on adjacent development parcels. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 46|Source: PDF pp. 46–47, printed pp. 38–39]]

### 1995: Fieldwork

Two inspections, a single-mound registration, and six test pits were reported in different parts of the site area. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 46|Source: PDF p. 46, printed p. 38]]

### 1992: Condition assessment

An archaeological inspection documented the site. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 46|Source: PDF p. 46, printed p. 38]]

### 1983: Site recording

The site was entered in the national archaeological record. [[Papers/finca-rosita-santa-ana#Page 42|Source: PDF pp. 42–43, printed pp. 34–35]]
