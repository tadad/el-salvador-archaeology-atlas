# Nuevo Lourdes Poniente

## Description

An excavated site west of Urbanización Nuevo Lourdes Extensión in Colón. The 2013 work documented three primary burials whose pits cut through the Ilopango TBJ deposit. The three human-bone samples calibrated to 678–862, 688–864, and 651–764 CE (2 sigma). The burials had offerings, and stone slabs or adobe blocks bounded parts of the graves. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 167|Source: PDF pp. 167–168, 172 (printed pp. 167–168, 172), ‘Nuevo Lourdes,’ ‘Método,’ and post-Ilopango discussion]]

## Studies

### 2014: Collection analysis

Human-bone samples were AMS dated and the deposits and chronology were reinterpreted. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 162|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF pp. 162–173]]
