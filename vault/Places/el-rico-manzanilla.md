# El Rico / Estero Manzanilla shell middens

## Description

Two shell middens were identified in the El Rico area; the source places one in Estero Manzanilla. The article discusses them among pre-Hispanic shell-midden precedents but gives no site-specific dating evidence. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 45|Source: PDF p. 45 (printed p. 45), ‘Antecedentes de concheros prehispánicos en El Salvador’]]

## Studies

### July 2000: Fieldwork

Fabio Amador's Gulf reconnaissance identified the El Rico and Manzanilla shell middens. No excavation is documented. It does not describe their deposits, contents, size, or associated artifacts. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 45|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF p. 45]]
