# Laguna Seca, Chalchuapa

## Overview

A low residential landscape around a former crater immediately east of Tazumal, part of Chalchuapa’s continuous settlement rather than a separate ceremonial center.

## Chronology

Material spans multiple periods of Chalchuapa’s occupation.

## How it was investigated

The Pennsylvania project conducted limited excavation in 1970 and catalogued platforms and deposits across the area.

## What archaeologists found

House-platform remains and ceramics demonstrated that dense residential occupation linked the better-known monumental groups.

## Research history
