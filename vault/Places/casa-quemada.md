# Casa Quemada

## Description

A roughly 9-hectare Late Classic settlement on a natural platform beside the Torola River. The 2013 project recorded 18 structures and three plazas. Excavators documented stone-and-earth architecture, fragmentary ceramics, obsidian, metate fragments, and a fired pit interpreted as a ceramic-firing oven. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 89|Source: PDF p. 89 (printed p. 89), article summary]]

## Studies

### 2016: Collection analysis

A projectile-point thesis analyzed 30 artifacts from Casa Quemada. [[Papers/2016-barrera-puntas-flecha-oriente#Page 56|Source: Análisis morfológico y funcional de las puntas de flecha del oriente de El Salvador, PDF pp. 56–58, 79–91]]

### 2013: Fieldwork

A rescue excavation investigated 14 of 18 structures and all plazas. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 89|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF pp. 89–113]]

### 2008–2009: Fieldwork

The regional survey discovered the site and opened test pits and trenches. In 2009, investigators opened test pits and trenches at Structure 6 and two plaza areas. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 64|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF pp. 64–69]]
