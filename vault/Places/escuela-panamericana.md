# Escuela Panamericana find locality

## Description

El Salvador's Ministry of Culture reported in July 2026 that pre-Hispanic remains had been found in 1985 during construction of Escuela Panamericana. The notice describes the older discoveries as similar to a newly investigated pre-Hispanic burial near San Salvador, but it does not identify the number or character of the school finds, their date, associated objects, recovery method, or an underlying report. This record therefore treats the school as a documented find locality rather than a bounded archaeological site or confirmed cemetery. [Source: Ministry of Culture, 21 July 2026, background section](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/realizan-extraccion-de-entierro-prehispanico-encontrado-en-antiguo-cuscatlan/) [Secondary report: Canal 12, 22 July 2026](https://www.canal12.com.sv/noticia/extraen-entierro-prehispanico-en-antiguo-cuscatlan/)

## Location and identification

The present Escuela Panamericana campus is at Calle El Carmen #1348, Colonia Escalón, San Salvador. The atlas marker uses the mapped school feature as a public-landmark proxy because the 2026 heritage notice publishes no archaeological coordinate, parcel plan, or discovery location within the property. The available sources do not independently establish that the 1985 construction exposure occurred on the exact parcel occupied by the present campus. [Source: Escuela Panamericana, current location](https://www.escuelapanamericana.org/locacion) [Source: OpenStreetMap way 400004183](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/400004183) [Mapped feature and coordinates](https://mapcarta.com/es/W400004183)

## Regional context

Longyear's 1944 national site list records pottery from San Antonio Abad, approximately 4 km northwest of central San Salvador, and from a Hacienda San Francisco on the southeastern slope of Volcán San Salvador. These entries establish older archaeological evidence in the broader sector but provide no basis for identifying either locality with the school discovery. [[Papers/longyear#Page 80|Source: PDF p. 80 (printed p. 79), San Antonio Abad and San Francisco entries]]

## Studies

### 2026: Retrospective public notice

The Ministry of Culture cited the 1985 Escuela Panamericana discoveries as historical context for a separate burial investigation. It did not publish a site name, registration number, boundary, chronology, artifact inventory, or citation to the original 1985 documentation. The wording supports a pre-Hispanic find at the school but does not demonstrate systematic excavation. [Source: Ministry of Culture, 21 July 2026](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/realizan-extraccion-de-entierro-prehispanico-encontrado-en-antiguo-cuscatlan/)

### 1985: Construction-period discovery

Pre-Hispanic remains were reportedly encountered during school construction. No public source located for this record documents whether archaeologists inspected, excavated, or recovered the exposed material, so the event is not classified as archaeological fieldwork. [Source: Ministry of Culture, retrospective notice](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/realizan-extraccion-de-entierro-prehispanico-encontrado-en-antiguo-cuscatlan/)
