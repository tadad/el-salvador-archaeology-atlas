# Finca Bolinas / Bolinas 1

## Description

Finca Bolinas was a private ranch about 6 km south of Chalchuapa where 95 clay figurines were reportedly found around the mid-twentieth century. An archival registration-card entry named Bolinas 1 may refer to the same locality, but the sources do not prove that identification. The figurine type is associated regionally with the Middle and Late Preclassic, while the archival card more broadly labels Bolinas 1 Preclassic and Classic. Neither source securely dates the discovery or deposit. ## Studies

### By 2024: Reinterpretation

A comparative study submitted and revised in 2024 reanalyzed the Finca Bolinas figurine collection and emphasized its insecure context; 2025 is the publication year. The circumstances and context of the Finca Bolinas discovery are uncertain, while the archival table gives no method or coordinate. The reconstructed marker follows only the article's statement that the ranch lay about 6 km south of Chalchuapa. The collection contains 93 figures interpreted as female and two possibly male. They vary from a few centimetres to more than 20 cm in height, use cream or reddish-brown paste, commonly retain white or orange slip, and often show elaborate hair, jewellery, or clothing. Some had string-articulated heads or limbs. Punctate eye treatment loosely unifies the broad Bolinas category, but the collection is not a secure in-situ tableau.
