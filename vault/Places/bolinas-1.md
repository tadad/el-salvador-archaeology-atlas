# Finca Bolinas / Bolinas 1

## Overview

Finca Bolinas was a private ranch about 6 km south of Chalchuapa where 95 clay figurines were reportedly found around the mid-twentieth century. An archival registration-card entry named Bolinas 1 may refer to the same locality, but the sources do not prove that identification.

## Chronology

The figurine type is associated regionally with the Middle and Late Preclassic. The archival card broadly labels Bolinas 1 Preclassic and Classic; neither source dates the discovery or deposit securely.

## How it was investigated

The circumstances and context of the Finca Bolinas discovery are uncertain, while the archival table gives no method or coordinate. The reconstructed marker follows only the article's statement that the ranch lay about 6 km south of Chalchuapa.

## What archaeologists found

The collection contains 93 figures interpreted as female and two possibly male. They vary from a few centimetres to more than 20 cm in height, use cream or reddish-brown paste, commonly retain white or orange slip, and often show elaborate hair, jewellery, or clothing. Some had string-articulated heads or limbs. Punctate eye treatment loosely unifies the broad Bolinas category, but the collection is not a secure in-situ tableau.

## Research history

### By 2024 — Reinterpretation

A comparative study submitted and revised in 2024 reanalyzed the Finca Bolinas figurine collection and emphasized its insecure context; 2025 is the publication year.

The article places private-ranch Finca Bolinas about 6 km south of Chalchuapa and reports that 95 figurines were found there around the mid-twentieth century, but stresses that their discovery circumstances and archaeological context are uncertain. Ninety-three represent females and two may be male; forms vary in size, paste, slip, paint, hair, jewellery, clothing, and occasional string articulation, while punctate eye treatment loosely unifies the broad type. Because the collection is not a secure context, it cannot establish an in-situ tableau. The paper also notes protruding abdomens on most complete figures as a possible pregnancy or postpartum reference, explicitly as indirect support for a speculative interpretation.
