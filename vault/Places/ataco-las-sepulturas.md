# Ataco

## Overview

Ataco is an excavated pre-Hispanic site with a mapped nuclear zone. The cited source does not publish its precise location or connect it to the name Las Sepulturas.

## Chronology

The project reported Preclassic and Postclassic evidence. Reconnaissance, mapping, and exploratory excavation took place in 2006–2007; work continued under new direction from 2008.

## How it was investigated

Researchers carried out archaeological reconnaissance, mapped the site's nuclear zone for the first time, and opened exploratory excavations.

## What archaeologists found

The later project studied jaguar heads and their spatial distribution. A 2025 synthesis also reports fragments of Bolinas-type figurines in structural fill, but gives no specimen count, unit, or primary depositional context.

## Research history

### 2024 — Reinterpretation

Ataco structural-fill figurines were incorporated into a revised comparative model of Bolinas figurines.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 1|2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 3]]

### 2008–2011 — Fieldwork

Under Federico Paredes, the continuing project studied Preclassic and Postclassic dynamics and jaguar-head spatial distribution; it was active when reported at the 2011 symposium.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 1|2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 9 (printed pp. 427, 435)]]

### 2006–2007 — Fieldwork

The initial project conducted reconnaissance, the first map of the nuclear zone, and exploratory excavations.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 9|2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 9 (printed p. 435)]]
