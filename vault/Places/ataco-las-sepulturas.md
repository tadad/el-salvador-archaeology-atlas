# Ataco

## Description

Ataco is an excavated pre-Hispanic site with a mapped nuclear zone. The cited source does not publish its precise location or connect it to the name Las Sepulturas. The later project studied jaguar heads and their spatial distribution. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 9|Source: PDF p. 9 (printed p. 435), recent-projects summary]]

## Studies

### 2024: Reinterpretation

Ataco structural-fill figurines were incorporated into a revised comparative model of Bolinas figurines. A 2025 synthesis also reports fragments of Bolinas-type figurines in structural fill, but gives no specimen count, unit, or primary depositional context. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 1|Source: 2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 3]]

### 2008–2011: Fieldwork

Under Federico Paredes, the continuing project studied Preclassic and Postclassic dynamics and jaguar-head spatial distribution; it was active when reported at the 2011 symposium. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 1|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 9 (printed pp. 427, 435)]]

### 2006–2007: Fieldwork

The initial project conducted reconnaissance, the first map of the nuclear zone, and exploratory excavations. The later project studied jaguar heads and their spatial distribution. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 9|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 9 (printed p. 435)]]
