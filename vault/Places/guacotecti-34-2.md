# Guacotecti (34-2)

## Description

A 2025 [[Organizations/ministerio-de-cultura|Ministry of Culture]] resolution identifies Guacotecti as registered archaeological site 34-2 and states that archaeological features have been reported there. That is the only published description located for the site. The resolution does not say whether the features are architecture, modified terrain, subsurface deposits, or another kind of evidence, and it lists no artifacts or chronology. [Resolution DA-128-2025, PDF pp. 53–54](https://www.transparencia.gob.sv/descarga_archivo.php?id=NjI5ODQ3)

## Nearby negative survey

The El Potrero development parcel used as the reference point for locating Guacotecti contained no archaeological material or artificial pre-Hispanic elevations. That negative survey applies to the development parcel, not to registered site 34-2.

## Location and uncertainty

The resolution places the site about 1.5 km southeast of the El Potrero project parcel in Cantón Izcatal, but that parcel could not be tied to a public map feature. The atlas therefore uses the center of Guacotecti as an openly labeled locality proxy, not as a reconstruction of the site coordinate.
