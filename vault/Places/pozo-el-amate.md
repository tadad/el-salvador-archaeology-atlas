# Pozo El Amate

## Description

A terrace southwest of Jocoaitique where a 1954 survey recorded surface obsidian chips. Visited between June 29 and July 2, 1954. The source does not date the obsidian chips. Numerous obsidian chips were found, but none was a recognizable tool. [[Papers/haberland_morazan#Page 1|Source: PDF pp. 1–2 (printed pp. 119–120), survey dates and Pozo El Amate site account]]

## Studies

### 29 June–2 July 1954: Fieldwork

Haberland visited the locality during the Morazán reconnaissance and recorded archaeological material. Haberland reported material visible on the surface; no excavation is documented. Numerous obsidian chips were found, but none was a recognizable tool. Three weathered sherds found elsewhere on the terrace were judged modern.
