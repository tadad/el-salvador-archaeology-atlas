# Palacio Nacional, San Salvador

## Description

Rescue work in the west corridor exposed three masonry walls between 0.8 and 2 metres deep and a 17.3-metre paved surface associated with an earlier building phase. [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 76|Source: PDF p. 76–77]]
