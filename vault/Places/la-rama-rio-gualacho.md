# La Rama / Río Gualacho

## Description

Two profiles near Puerto El Triunfo preserved human footprints and pottery in buried layers. A later synthesis tentatively dated the La Rama footprints to about 1500 BCE or earlier and Gualacho to no later than 1000 BCE; pottery from nearby La Rama mounds was tentatively dated around AD 1200. La Rama preserved human and animal tracks beneath the surface and a few sherds in its upper layer. [[Papers/haberland_y_grebe#Page 1|Source: PDF pp. 1–4 (printed pp. 282–285)]]

## Studies

### 1960: Reinterpretation

Haberland revised the ceramic sequence and tentative chronology for La Rama and Río Gualacho. [[Papers/haberland_sequences#Page 6|Source: haberland_sequences.pdf, PDF p. 6]]

### 1955: Fieldwork

A geological and archaeological reconnaissance documented profiles, cuts, pottery, and footprints. Researchers cut into the road banks at La Rama to expose footprints and documented a stratigraphic profile at Río Gualacho. They also examined pottery collected from a nearby mound excavated by the landowner in 1953. [[Papers/haberland_y_grebe#Page 1|Source: haberland_y_grebe.pdf, PDF pp. 1–4]]
