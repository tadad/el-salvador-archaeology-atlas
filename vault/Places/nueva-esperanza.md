# Nueva Esperanza

## Description

An excavated site in the Nueva Esperanza community of Bajo Lempa, with burials preserved in a lacustrine setting and archaeological deposits beneath Ilopango ash. Burials 2–4 included a child, a young adult man with filed teeth and a rotated canine, and an adult woman without third molars and with a possibly filed canine. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 66|Source: PDF pp. 66–68 (printed pp. 66–68), Nueva Esperanza burials 2–4 and fig. 4]]

## Studies

### 2014–2015: Collection analysis

Burials 2–4 were selected for osteological and funerary reanalysis, with the final analytical season documented in 2015. A later osteological study examined Burials 2–4 while their analysis was still in progress. Burials 2–4 included a child, a young adult man with filed teeth and a rotated canine, and an adult woman without third molars and with a possibly filed canine. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 60, 66–69]]

### 2011: Fieldwork

A rescue excavation documented burials and their associated archaeological contexts. The 2011 excavation documented a stratigraphic profile and recovered human bone and charcoal samples. Another excavated woman was buried beneath the ash with 19 ceramic offerings. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 165|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF pp. 165–166]]
