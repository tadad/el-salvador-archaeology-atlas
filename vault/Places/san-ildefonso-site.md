# San Ildefonso archaeological site

## Description

A mound-and-plaza site associated with San Ildefonso, part of the dense archaeological landscape overlooking the upper Lempa. Researchers recorded an elevated plaza and associated mounds, evidence for planned public space in the San Ildefonso locality. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 40|Source: PDF p. 40 (printed p. 29), San Idelfonso site description]]

## Studies

### 2006–2008: Site recording

Atlas reconnaissance confirmed an elevated plaza, monumental mounds, and a domestic area; the exact visit year is unstated. The atlas project documented the layout and surface architecture; the cited section does not establish an intensive excavation program.
