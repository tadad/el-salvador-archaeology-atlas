# Abrigo de Los Fierros

## Description

A roughly triangular rock-art shelter in volcanic tuff, with most paintings on its east wall near the entrance. The images are undated and mostly represent animals; the report does not assign them to an archaeological period. [[Papers/1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente#Page 4|Source: PDF pp. 4–5 (printed pp. 663–664), rapid-visit statement and ‘El Abrigo Rocoso de Los Fierros’]]

## Studies

### April 1996: Site recording

A ten-day eastern rock-art project documented the shelter and its paintings. During the 1996 regional project, the team made a brief description and photographed the paintings; no excavation is documented. Most images represent animals. The report identifies a red human figure, a yellow hand, a green monkey with yellow around it, two crabs, possible human-animal figures, signs, and red dots; green is the predominant color. [[Papers/1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente#Page 1|Source: 1998-coladan-pinturas-rupestres-oriente.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 6]]
