# El Mapache

## Overview

A newly registered site from the 2007 reconnaissance of the southern Ahuachapán coastal plain.

## Chronology

Preclassic survey context; recorded in 2007.

## How it was investigated

Surveyors identified surface cultural material and registered the locality by GPS.

## What archaeologists found

The site added another point to the dense early-settlement network surrounding Atalaya, although no controlled excavation is summarized.

## Research history

### 2007 — Site recording

The southern Ahuachapán reconnaissance newly registered El Mapache.
