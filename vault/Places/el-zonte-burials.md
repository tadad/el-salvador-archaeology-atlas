# El Zonte burials

## Overview

Two burials were reported from a private beachfront parcel at El Zonte. The source describes Burial 1’s later osteological analysis but supplies little detail about Burial 2.

## Chronology

Burial 1 is tentatively dated to the Late Classic (600–900 CE) through associated material. The cited source does not date Burial 2.

## How it was investigated

Burial 1 was found accidentally and lost its original archaeological context. In 1998 Roberto Gallardo archaeologically excavated Burial 2, but the cited source does not describe the excavation method.

## What archaeologists found

The later study identifies Burial 1 as a young adult man with bilobed cranial modification, dental disease, and Schmorl’s nodes. The cited source does not describe Burial 2’s remains or associated finds.

## Research history

### 2015 — Collection analysis

El Zonte Burial 1 was included in an osteological and paleopathological study season.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 60, 63–64]]

### 2009 — Archival synthesis

The burial report was reedited; this was documentary work rather than a new excavation.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 55|anales-56.pdf, PDF p. 55]]

### 1998 — Fieldwork

Gallardo archaeologically investigated and excavated Burial 2.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 55|anales-56.pdf, PDF p. 55]]
