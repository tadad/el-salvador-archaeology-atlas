# El Zonte burials

## Description

Two burials were reported from a private beachfront parcel at El Zonte. The source describes Burial 1’s later osteological analysis but supplies little detail about Burial 2. Burial 1 is tentatively dated to the Late Classic (600–900 CE) through associated material. The cited source does not date Burial 2. The later study identifies Burial 1 as a young adult man with bilobed cranial modification, dental disease, and Schmorl’s nodes. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 61|Source: PDF pp. 61, 63–64 (printed pp. 61, 63–64), El Zonte case study and fig. 2]]

## Studies

### 2015: Collection analysis

El Zonte Burial 1 was included in an osteological and paleopathological study season. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 60, 63–64]]

### 2009: Archival synthesis

The burial report was reedited; this was documentary work rather than a new excavation. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 55|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF p. 55]]

### 1998: Fieldwork

Gallardo archaeologically investigated and excavated Burial 2. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 55|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF p. 55]]
