# Pacún

## Description

Pacún is a buried Early Classic pre-Hispanic settlement in a quebrada in Tonacatepeque. Excavations identified three earthen structures or platforms and two possible buried structures, together with ceramics, figurines, obsidian, ground stone, and preserved plant remains including maize and beans. The site was discovered by a local resident in 2022. [[Papers/2024-flores-riqueza-arqueologica-centro-san-salvador#Page 3|Source: PDF p. 3, published archaeological synthesis]] [Source: Ministry of Culture, 14 September 2022, inspection and preliminary chronology](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/patrimonio-emite-declaratoria-en-favor-de-hallazgo-en-tonacatepeque/) [Source: Ministry of Culture, 30 October 2023, excavation results](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/presentan-hallazgos-del-sitio-arqueologico-pacun/)

## Studies

### 2023: Excavation

Ministry archaeologists and Mexican specialists excavated at the site in 2023. At the time of the public presentation, INAH laboratory work intended to date ceramics and organic material was ongoing. [Source: Ministry of Culture, 30 October 2023](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/presentan-hallazgos-del-sitio-arqueologico-pacun/)
