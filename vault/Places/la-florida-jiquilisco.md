# La Florida (Jiquilisco)

## Description

A settlement south of the coastal highway in the Jiquilisco district, selected as one of the eastern atlas project’s intensive test sites. Late/Terminal Preclassic and Late Classic–Terminal, based on the ceramic groups tabulated by the Atlas. The excavation documented buried occupation across the site and recovered ceramics for regional comparison. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 52|Source: PDF p. 52 (printed p. 41), site description and Table 4.4]]

## Studies

### 2006–2008: Fieldwork

The Atlas project opened 36 test pits and analyzed recovered material; the exact field season is unstated. Thirty-six test pits made La Florida one of the more extensively sampled localities in the atlas field program. The excavation documented buried occupation across the site and recovered ceramics for regional comparison. Its importance lies in the breadth of the sample and the settlement evidence it preserved. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 52|Source: Atlas Arqueológico de la Región de Oriente de El Salvador, PDF p. 52]]
