# Concepción Ramírez I

## Description

Concepción Ramírez I is a hydraulic indigo work recorded during the 2011 archaeological reconnaissance of colonial indigo-production infrastructure in San Vicente and La Paz. The publication supplies a coordinate, construction observations, photographs, and a condition assessment. [[Papers/2012-erquicia-obrajes-anil-san-vicente-la-paz#Page 32|Source: PDF p. 32]]

## Studies

### 2011: Archaeological register

The project visited candidate localities between 6 June and 12 July 2011 and recorded surviving indigo works through surface reconnaissance. This record represents the named installation, not the full surrounding historic property.
