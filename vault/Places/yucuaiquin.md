# Yucuaiquín

## Description

A potential archaeological locality somewhere in or around modern Yucuaiquín. An early report stated that the town stood over pre-Hispanic remains, and a later survey verified an unspecified archaeological find. The cited sources document no excavation or site boundary. ### May 2006–July 2008: Fieldwork

The Atlas Arqueológico del Oriente project field-verified Yucuaiquín; the source does not state the exact site visit date. No excavation is documented. Researchers reported the locality and later field-checked it, but the published sources provide no investigation details. An unspecified portable archaeological find. No structures, pottery types, burials, or excavated deposits are described.
