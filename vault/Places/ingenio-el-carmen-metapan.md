# Ingenio El Carmen, Metapán

## Overview

A water-powered ironworks north of Metapán whose surviving walls supported a detailed reconstruction of the production building.

## Chronology

Colonial iron industry.

## How it was investigated

Researchers combined measured architecture, comparison with Iberian ironworks and digital three-dimensional reconstruction.

## What archaeologists found

The building plan clarifies how furnace, water power, work areas and roofed industrial space operated as one mechanical system.

## Research history

### 2021 — Reinterpretation

A technological-history study produced a three-dimensional reconstruction of the ironworks; it reports no new field visit.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 84|identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 84]]

### August 2008 — Site recording

PAHES mapped and documented the ironworks during its second field phase.

**Evidence:** 2010-erquicia-sitios-historicos-fase-2.pdf, PDF pp. 3–4, 12 (printed pp. 841–842, 850)
