# Ingenio El Carmen, Metapán

## Overview

A surveyed water-powered ironworks in cantón El Panal, north of Metapán. A later study used the surviving layout for a hypothetical digital reconstruction.

## Chronology

The ironworks operated before 1770 and probably ceased production between 1860 and the end of the nineteenth century.

## How it was investigated

PAHES mapped the surviving remains in August 2008. A 2021 study digitized site data and compared the layout with Iberian ironworks to create a hypothetical three-dimensional reconstruction; neither source documents controlled excavation.

## What archaeologists found

The 2008 report describes foundations, walls, retaining walls, columns, two channels and water-drop basins supplied by the nearby Chimalapa River.

## Research history

### 2021 — Reinterpretation

A technological-history study used bibliographic research, field observations and comparison with Iberian ironworks to create a hypothetical three-dimensional reconstruction. It does not date its field visits.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 84|identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 84–86 (printed pp. 84–86)]]

### August 2008 — Site recording

PAHES recorded the ironworks during its 2008 second phase and published an August 2008 plan.

**Evidence:** 2010-erquicia-pahes-segunda-fase.pdf, PDF pp. 4–5, 9, 12 (printed pp. 842–843, 847, 850)
