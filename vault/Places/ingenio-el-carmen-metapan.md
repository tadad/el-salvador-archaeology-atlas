# Ingenio El Carmen, Metapán

## Description

A surveyed water-powered ironworks in cantón El Panal, north of Metapán. A later study used the surviving layout for a hypothetical digital reconstruction. The ironworks operated before 1770 and probably ceased production between 1860 and the end of the nineteenth century. The 2008 report describes foundations, walls, retaining walls, columns, two channels and water-drop basins supplied by the nearby Chimalapa River. [Source: PDF pp. 4–5 (printed pp. 842–843), Ingenio El Carmen entry; PDF p. 9 (printed p. 847), phase summary; PDF p. 12 (printed p. 850), Figure 3 site plan](https://www.asociaciontikal.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/63.09-Erquicia_09.pdf#page=4) [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 84|Source: PDF pp. 84–86 (printed pp. 84–86), Images 4–5 and “Reconstrucción tridimensional”]]

## Studies

### 2021: Reinterpretation

A technological-history study digitized site data and used bibliographic research, field observations, and comparison with Iberian ironworks to create a hypothetical three-dimensional reconstruction. It does not date its field visits or document controlled excavation. [[Papers/identidades-18-arqueologia#Page 84|Source: identidades-18-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 84–86 (printed pp. 84–86)]]

### August 2008: Site recording

PAHES recorded the ironworks during its 2008 second phase and published an August 2008 plan. The 2008 report describes foundations, walls, retaining walls, columns, two channels and water-drop basins supplied by the nearby Chimalapa River.
