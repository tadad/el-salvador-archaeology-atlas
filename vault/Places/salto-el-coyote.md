# Salto El Coyote

## Description

A compact settlement near the San Lorenzo reservoir whose paired mounds form a ballcourt. It adds a second ceremonial ballgame setting to the Estanzuelas–San Ildefonso cluster. Late Classic–Terminal, about 625–1000 CE in the Atlas chronology. Researchers documented a small ballcourt, a connected platform, a separate mound, and associated features. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 49|Source: PDF pp. 49–50 (printed pp. 38–39), site description, maps, methods, and ceramic chronology]]

## Studies

### 2017: Reinterpretation

A ballcourt study reassessed the site's architecture and landscape; it reports no new excavation. [Source: 2017-canchas-juego-pelota-oriente.pdf, PDF pp. 159–163](https://biblioteca.utec.edu.sv/siab/virtual/tesis/941001110.pdf#page=159)

### 2006–2008: Fieldwork

The Atlas project mapped the site, tested it, and analyzed its ceramics; the exact field season is unstated. The atlas team mapped the site and used test excavations to sample deposits associated with its plaza and ballcourt architecture. Four test pits and surface collection produced 1,883 ceramic sherds and 68 lithic fragments; the diagnostic ceramics were assigned to the Lepa phase. [[Papers/2009-amador-atlas-arqueologico-oriente#Page 49|Source: Atlas Arqueológico de la Región de Oriente de El Salvador, PDF pp. 49–50]]
