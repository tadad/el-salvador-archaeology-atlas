# El Primo, Colón

## Description

An archaeology-department registration card preserves an unverified lead named El Primo in Colón municipality. The card tentatively asks whether there was a lidded funerary urn with a jar beside it. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 47|Source: PDF pp. 47, 50–51 (printed pp. 47, 50–51), registration-card caveat and El Primo entry]]

## Studies

### 2016: Archival synthesis

A national burial review reassessed the archaeology-department registration card and its evidentiary limits. The card tentatively asks whether there was a lidded funerary urn with a jar beside it. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 47|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 47, 50–51]]
