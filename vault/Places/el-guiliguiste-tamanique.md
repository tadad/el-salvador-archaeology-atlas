# El Güiligüishte

## Description

El Güiligüishte comprises ten visible structures about 270 metres southeast of El Tecolote. The survey recorded surface ceramics, obsidian, and/or ground stone and mapped the visible architecture. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 145|Source: PDF p. 145, site description]] [[Papers/2010-torres-arqueologia-salvador-1992-2007#Page 115|Source: PDF pp. 115–116, survey summary]]

## Studies

### 2007–2017: Survey and community archaeology

A systematic survey of approximately 400 manzanas recorded El Güiligüishte as one of four sites, measured and photographed the visible structures, and placed protected buffers around them. A later public-archaeology article confirms that all four were delimited on the cooperative's plan.
