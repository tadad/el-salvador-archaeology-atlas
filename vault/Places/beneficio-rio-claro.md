# Beneficio Río Claro

## Description

A surveyed historic coffee mill on Finca Río Claro, about 2 km north of Quezaltepeque. The report describes a coffee-mill structure with machinery, basins, channels, drying patios, and administrative structures. [[Papers/2009-erquicia-sitios-arqueologicos-historicos#Page 2|Source: PDF pp. 2–3, 11 (printed pp. 152–153, 161), project field phase and methodology and Beneficio Río Claro entry]]

## Studies

### August–December 2007: Site recording

PAHES documented and inventoried the coffee works during its first field phase. The report lists project-wide methods but does not say which were used here, and it documents no controlled excavation. The report describes a coffee-mill structure with machinery, basins, channels, drying patios, and administrative structures. Its historical discussion attributes turbine-powered wet processing to Río Claro, but does not establish that the early equipment survives.
