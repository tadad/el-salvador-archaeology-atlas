# Beneficio Río Claro

## Overview

A surveyed historic coffee mill on Finca Río Claro, about 2 km north of Quezaltepeque.

## Chronology

The report associates the complex with coffee production from the late nineteenth century through the time of the 2007 survey; it does not date individual features.

## How it was investigated

PAHES visited and documented Río Claro during its August–December 2007 field phase. The report lists project-wide methods but does not say which were used here, and it documents no controlled excavation.

## What archaeologists found

The report describes a coffee-mill structure with machinery, basins, channels, drying patios, and administrative structures. Its historical discussion attributes turbine-powered wet processing to Río Claro, but does not establish that the early equipment survives.

## Research history

### August–December 2007 — Site recording

PAHES documented and inventoried the coffee works during its first field phase.
