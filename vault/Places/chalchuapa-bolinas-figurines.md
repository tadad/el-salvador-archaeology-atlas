# Chalchuapa (Bolinas-type figurine finds)

## Overview

A regional evidence point for Bolinas-type figurine fragments somewhere within greater Chalchuapa; the 2025 article does not assign them to a particular component site.

## Chronology

The Bolinas tradition is broadly Middle and Late Preclassic. The article does not date the Chalchuapa specimens or the work that recovered them.

## How it was investigated

The article synthesizes earlier Chalchuapa research rather than reporting new fieldwork. Most cited fragments came from surface, structural fill, mixed deposits, or other secondary contexts.

## What archaeologists found

Fragmentary Bolinas-type figurines are reported, without a specimen count or component-site attribution. In a separate comparison, the authors cite Chalchuapa as evidence that monumental building can occur in communal rather than ruler-centered contexts.

## Research history

### 2024 — Reinterpretation

Chalchuapa finds were reassessed within a revised regional model of Bolinas figurines.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 1|2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF pp. 1–3]]

### 2009 — Collection analysis

A bachelor's dissertation classified Preclassic figurines from Casa Blanca and Laguna Cuzcachapa within the broader Chalchuapa area.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 14|2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 14]]

### Circa 1960s — Site recording

Fragmentary Bolinas figurines were recovered at Chalchuapa, primarily from surface, fill, or mixed deposits; the exact seasons are not stated.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 3|2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 3]]
