# Chalchuapa (Bolinas-type figurine finds)

## Description

A regional evidence point for Bolinas-type figurine fragments somewhere within greater Chalchuapa; the 2025 article does not assign them to a particular component site. The Bolinas tradition is broadly Middle and Late Preclassic. The article does not date the Chalchuapa specimens or the work that recovered them. Fragmentary Bolinas-type figurines are reported, without a specimen count or component-site attribution. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 2|Source: PDF pp. 2–3, 13 (printed pp. 781–782, 792), regional Bolinas finds and sociopolitical comparison]]

### 2009: Collection analysis

A bachelor's dissertation classified Preclassic figurines from Casa Blanca and Laguna Cuzcachapa within the broader Chalchuapa area. [[Papers/2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines#Page 14|Source: 2025-szymanski-prejs-san-isidro-figurines.pdf, PDF p. 14]]
