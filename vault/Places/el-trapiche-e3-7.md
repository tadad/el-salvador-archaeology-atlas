# El Trapiche (mound E3-7)

## Description

Mound E3-7 is an excavated part of El Trapiche in Chalchuapa. Researchers reported 33 burials within the mound. The 33 individuals had been buried within the mound. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 4|Source: PDF p. 4 (printed p. 430), El Trapiche E3-7 summary]] [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|Source: PDF p. 60 (printed p. 60), “El Entierro 78-8B-SA-1 de El Trapiche”]]

## Studies

### 2015: Collection analysis

Burial 78-8B-SA-1 was examined in the MUNA paleopathology study season. Manuel López and William Fowler excavated mound E3-7. [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 60–63, 71]]

### 1977–1978: Fieldwork

Rescue excavation of Structure E3-7 recovered thirty-three individuals. The cited summaries do not describe the excavation methods. The 33 individuals had been buried within the mound. Fowler interpreted the context as human sacrifice; a later study says the bodies were prone with hands and feet tied and were placed collectively in three successive events. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 4|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 4 (printed p. 430)]] [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|Source: anales-56.pdf, PDF p. 60]]

### 1954: Fieldwork

Coe conducted an excavation campaign at El Trapiche. [[Papers/coe_trapiche#Page 1|Source: coe_trapiche.pdf, PDF pp. 1–4]]

### 1942: Fieldwork

The El Trapiche mound group was mapped and tested. [[Papers/longyear#Page 24|Source: longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 24–26 (printed pp. 17–19)]]
