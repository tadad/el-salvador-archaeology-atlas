# El Trapiche (mound E3-7)

## Overview

Mound E3-7 is an excavated part of El Trapiche in Chalchuapa. Researchers reported 33 burials within the mound.

## Chronology

One documented burial is assigned to the Late Preclassic Caynac phase, 100 BCE–100 CE. Rescue excavation took place in 1977–1978.

## How it was investigated

Manuel López and William Fowler excavated mound E3-7. The cited summaries do not describe the excavation methods.

## What archaeologists found

The 33 individuals had been buried within the mound. Fowler interpreted the context as human sacrifice; a later study says the bodies were prone with hands and feet tied and were placed collectively in three successive events.

## Research history

### 2015 — Collection analysis

Burial 78-8B-SA-1 was examined in the MUNA paleopathology study season.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-56#Page 60|anales-56.pdf, PDF pp. 60–63, 71]]

### 1977–1978 — Fieldwork

Rescue excavation of Structure E3-7 recovered thirty-three individuals.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 4|2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 4 (printed p. 430); anales-56.pdf, PDF p. 60]]

### 1954 — Fieldwork

Coe conducted an excavation campaign at El Trapiche.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/coe_trapiche#Page 1|coe_trapiche.pdf, PDF pp. 1–4]]

### 1942 — Fieldwork

The El Trapiche mound group was mapped and tested.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/longyear#Page 24|longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 24–26 (printed pp. 17–19)]]
