# Texistepeque Group I

## Overview

A reported mound group north of Texistepeque and east of the highway to Metapán.

## Chronology

Undated; the report gives no occupation date or cultural assignment.

## How it was investigated

The western reconnaissance records a visit sometime in 1940–1942 and describes the group, but gives no site-specific visit date or field methods.

## What archaeologists found

About ten mounds were reported, including three about 15 m square and 5.5 m high. None appeared to have been excavated.

## Research history

### 1940–1942 — Fieldwork

Boggs recorded the northern mound group during the western reconnaissance; the exact visit year is unstated.
