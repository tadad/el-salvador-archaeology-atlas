# Texistepeque Group I

## Description

A reported mound group north of Texistepeque and east of the highway to Metapán. Undated; the report gives no occupation date or cultural assignment. About ten mounds were reported, including three about 15 m square and 5.5 m high. ### 1940–1942: Fieldwork

Boggs recorded the northern mound group during the western reconnaissance; the exact visit year is unstated. The western reconnaissance records a visit sometime in 1940–1942 and describes the group, but gives no site-specific visit date or field methods. About ten mounds were reported, including three about 15 m square and 5.5 m high. None appeared to have been excavated.
