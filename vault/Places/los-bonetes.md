# Los Bonetes

## Description

A surveyed hill with two mesa-like tops on the Honduran frontier north of Carolina. Archaeological material was recorded in the saddle and on the northern top. The archaeological material is undated. Field notes recorded many obsidian chips, blades, a point, and very few sherds in the 701 m saddle. [[Papers/haberland_morazan#Page 1|Source: PDF p. 1 (printed p. 119) and PDF p. 5 (printed p. 123), reconnaissance dates and Los Bonetes account]] [[Papers/anales-53#Page 67|Source: PDF p. 67 (printed p. 67), archaeological background]]

## Studies

### 1972: Site recording

The Department of Archaeology entered a formal site card; the source does not state the field method. [[Papers/anales-53#Page 67|Source: anales-53.pdf, PDF p. 67]]

### 29 June–2 July 1954: Fieldwork

Haberland documented Los Bonetes during the Morazán reconnaissance. The report describes surface finds but no controlled excavation. Field notes recorded many obsidian chips, blades, a point, and very few sherds in the 701 m saddle. Obsidian chips were also found on the 740 m northern top. [[Papers/haberland_morazan#Page 1|Source: haberland_morazan.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 5]]
