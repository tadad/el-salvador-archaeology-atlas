# Madreselva

## Description

A rescue-excavated site within the Madre Selva development in Antiguo Cuscatlán. The report documents a Late Classic village and two Postclassic groups of structures. Late Classic remains included wattle-and-daub dwellings, refuse pits, and burials near houses, commonly with ceramic vessels. [[Papers/1994-amaroli-hermes-velasquez-antiguo-cuscatlan#Page 2|Source: PDF pp. 2–3 (printed pp. 528–529); Figure 2 on PDF p. 8 (printed p. 534)]] [[Papers/2025-flores-manzano-despues-ilopango#Page 14|Source: PDF pp. 14–18 (printed pp. 24–28), Madre Selva synthesis]]

## Studies

### 2024–2025: Archival synthesis

A new synthesis reexamined published and unpublished rescue records and presented an explicitly hypothetical reconstruction of the monumental groups. [[Papers/2025-flores-manzano-despues-ilopango#Page 1|Source: 2025-flores-manzano-despues-ilopango.pdf, PDF pp. 1, 14–29, 40–41]]

### July 2024: Condition assessment

Flores Manzano revisited the preservation zone, recorded a possible additional mound and relocated foundation, and made a Polycam/iPad LiDAR model; the structural identification is tentative. [[Papers/2025-flores-manzano-despues-ilopango#Page 38|Source: 2025-flores-manzano-despues-ilopango.pdf, PDF pp. 38–40 (printed pp. 48–50)]]

### 2013: Collection analysis

A thesis analyzed Copador vessels and their offering context. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 10|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 10–39]]

### 1998: Reinterpretation

Madreselva stratigraphy was used to corroborate the position and source of the Cuzcatan tephra. [[Papers/milpas#Page 9|Source: milpas.pdf, PDF p. 9]]

### 1992: Fieldwork

Rescue excavation investigated the site during urban development. A development-led rescue program excavated the site. [[Papers/1994-amaroli-hermes-velasquez-antiguo-cuscatlan#Page 1|Source: 1994-amaroli-hermes-velasquez-antiguo-cuscatlan.pdf, PDF pp. 1–8]]
