# Copapayo

## Description

Three mounds were recorded in a cleared field near the former Copapayo railroad station and Santa Tecla–Sonsonate highway. Undated; the report gives no occupation date or cultural assignment. The report records three mounds, one fairly large, and numerous potsherds on the surrounding surface.
