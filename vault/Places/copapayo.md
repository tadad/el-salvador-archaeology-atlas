# Copapayo

## Overview

Three mounds were recorded in a cleared field near the former Copapayo railroad station and Santa Tecla–Sonsonate highway.

## Chronology

Undated; the report gives no occupation date or cultural assignment.

## How it was investigated

Boggs’s western survey visited and described Copapayo sometime in 1940–1942, but gives no site-specific visit date or field methods. The report includes a sketch map; no controlled excavation is documented.

## What archaeologists found

The report records three mounds, one fairly large, and numerous potsherds on the surrounding surface.

## Research history

### 1940–1942 — Fieldwork

Boggs mapped three mounds and noted surface pottery; the exact visit year is unstated.
