# Tazumal

## Description

An excavated monumental precinct at Chalchuapa centered on Mounds 1 and 2. The cited publications document Late Classic architecture and an Early Postclassic addition dated to about 900–1200 CE. Excavation recorded repeated rebuilding of Mound 1, four post-construction burials, stone and obsidian objects, and distinct early and later pottery deposits. [[Papers/2007-erquicia-los-gavilanes#Page 2|Source: PDF p. 2 (printed p. 855), ‘El Postclásico en Chalchuapa’]] [[Papers/longyear#Page 61|Source: PDF pp. 61–63 (printed pp. 59–61), architecture, stone objects, burials, and pottery]]

## Studies

### 1921: Early interpretation

Lardé recorded an active disagreement over whether the name Tazumal and Chalchuapa’s major archaeological remains should be classified as Nahua or Maya. The passage is useful evidence for the history of interpretation, not a reliable modern cultural attribution. [[Papers/larde-1921-poblacion-el-salvador#Page 22|Source: PDF p. 22 (printed p. 32)]]

### 2015: Reinterpretation

A technical study reassessed restoration history, collapse causes, and the architectural sequence of the Edificio de las Columnas. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 131|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 131–140]]

### 2013: Collection analysis

A thesis analyzed Copador vessel contexts, forms, and chronology. [[Papers/anales-54#Page 10|Source: anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 10–39]]

### 2010–2012: Fieldwork

Project Tazumal investigated site topography and architectural development. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 9|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 9 (printed p. 435)]]

### 2004–2008: Fieldwork

Project Tazumal and collapse-response work combined archaeological investigation and restoration. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 8|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 8–9 (printed pp. 434–435)]]

### 1966–1970: Fieldwork

The University of Pennsylvania Chalchuapa Project included Tazumal in regional and site research. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 3|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 3 (printed p. 429)]]

### 1943–1953: Fieldwork

Boggs conducted repeated excavation and restoration seasons in 1943–1945, 1948–1950, and 1951–1953. [[Papers/tzunpame_boggs_tazumal#Page 2|Source: tzunpame_boggs_tazumal.pdf, PDF pp. 2–5]]; [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 2|Source: 2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 2 (printed p. 428)]]

### 1942: Fieldwork

The first documented tests used exploratory excavation and transit mapping to examine Mounds 1 and 2 in April–May 1942. Excavation recorded repeated rebuilding of Mound 1, four post-construction burials, stone and obsidian objects, and distinct early and later pottery deposits. [[Papers/longyear#Page 58|Source: longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 58–59 (printed pp. 51–52)]]
