# Tazumal

## Overview

An excavated monumental precinct at Chalchuapa centered on Mounds 1 and 2.

## Chronology

The cited publications document Late Classic architecture and an Early Postclassic addition dated to about 900–1200 CE.

## How it was investigated

Exploratory excavation and transit mapping examined Mounds 1 and 2 in April–May 1942. After part of Structure B1-2 collapsed in 2004, later projects combined excavation, restoration, topographic survey, and study of architectural development through 2012.

## What archaeologists found

Excavation recorded repeated rebuilding of Mound 1, four post-construction burials, stone and obsidian objects, and distinct early and later pottery deposits.

## Research history

### 2015 — Reinterpretation

A technical study reassessed restoration history, collapse causes, and the architectural sequence of the Edificio de las Columnas.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-54#Page 131|anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 131–140]]

### 2013 — Collection analysis

A thesis analyzed Copador vessel contexts, forms, and chronology.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/anales-54#Page 10|anales-54.pdf, PDF pp. 10–39]]

### 2010–2012 — Fieldwork

Project Tazumal investigated site topography and architectural development.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 9|2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 9 (printed p. 435)]]

### 2004–2008 — Fieldwork

Project Tazumal and collapse-response work combined archaeological investigation and restoration.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 8|2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF pp. 8–9 (printed pp. 434–435)]]

### 1966–1970 — Fieldwork

The University of Pennsylvania Chalchuapa Project included Tazumal in regional and site research.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 3|2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 3 (printed p. 429)]]

### 1943–1953 — Fieldwork

Boggs conducted repeated excavation and restoration seasons in 1943–1945, 1948–1950, and 1951–1953.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/tzunpame_boggs_tazumal#Page 2|tzunpame_boggs_tazumal.pdf, PDF pp. 2–5]]; [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 2|2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia.pdf, PDF p. 2 (printed p. 428)]]

### 1942 — Fieldwork

Exploratory excavation opened the first documented tests.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/longyear#Page 58|longyear.pdf, PDF pp. 58–59 (printed pp. 51–52)]]

### tzunpame_boggs_tazumal.pdf

**Citation:** PDF pp. 2–5 (printed pp. 116–122), early excavation results and interpretation

Summarizes Classic and Postclassic dating evidence while warning that the ethnic evidence is contradictory and does not securely identify Tazumal as Pipil or Pokoman Maya. This supports leaving the culture filter empty.
