# Ciudad Vieja

## Description

Ciudad Vieja is the excavated site of the first permanent Villa de San Salvador, an early Spanish colonial town about 10 kilometres south of Suchitoto. The settlement was founded in 1528 and probably abandoned in 1545, although later research suggests that occupation may have continued beyond that date. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 7|Source: PDF p. 7 (printed p. 433)]]

## Studies

### 2011: Reinterpretation

A site monograph presented and re-examined evidence for the town’s architecture and cultural landscape. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 8|Source: PDF p. 8 (printed p. 434)]]

### 2009: Collection analysis

A doctoral study compared household assemblages across the site. It used that variation to examine relations among the different ethnic groups that occupied the early colonial town. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 8|Source: PDF p. 8 (printed p. 434)]]

### 2007: Ceramic and landscape studies

One doctoral study analyzed Ciudad Vieja’s ceramics and their evidence for culture contact and social change. A separate publication used the project’s geophysical data to interpret the town as an archaeological landscape. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 6|Landscape-study source: PDF p. 6 (printed p. 432)]] [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 8|Ceramic-study source: PDF p. 8 (printed p. 434)]]

### 2000–2005: Excavation and survey

Repeated field seasons investigated the settlement. During 2002–2003, researchers combined magnetic gradiometry, electrical conductivity, magnetic susceptibility, GIS, and excavation; conductivity and susceptibility proved effective enough that the planned ground-penetrating radar survey was unnecessary. The geophysical work covered about 20 hectares, mapped buried rooms and a street south of the Plaza Mayor, and guided excavation of Structure 3D2, where excavators tested the anomaly map against the surviving architecture. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 7|Field-season source: PDF p. 7 (printed p. 433)]] [[Papers/2004-fowler-et-al-ciudad-vieja-teledeteccion#Page 3|2002–2003 source: PDF pp. 3–8 (printed pp. 679–684)]]

### 1996: Fieldwork

The Ciudad Vieja archaeological project began its investigation of the site. [[Papers/2012-escamilla-fowler-practica-arqueologia#Page 7|Source: PDF p. 7 (printed p. 433)]]
