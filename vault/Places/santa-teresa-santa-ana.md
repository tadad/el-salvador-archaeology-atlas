# Finca Santa Teresa

## Description

About twelve mounds were recorded in the coffee plantation at Finca Santa Teresa, including one described as among El Salvador’s largest. The report gives no occupation date or cultural assignment. Surface finds included figurine fragments and sculptured stone heads. [[Papers/longyear#Page 16|Source: PDF p. 16 (printed p. 9) and PDF p. 29 (printed p. 22), reconnaissance scope and Santa Teresa entry]]

## Studies

### 1940–1942: Fieldwork

During his 1940–1942 western field surveys, Boggs visited Santa Teresa and recorded twelve mounds and surface finds; the exact visit year is unstated. The report says no excavation had been done. Surface finds included figurine fragments and sculptured stone heads, and an eroded mound section indicated adobe construction.
