# Las Victorias, Chalchuapa

## Overview

A group of low platforms and abundant surface pottery on the former Finca Las Victorias, east of Chalchuapa.

## Chronology

Pre-Hispanic, otherwise undated in the cited source.

## How it was investigated

Field survey recorded the visible remains, and a museum expedition explored the group in 1942–1943. The Pennsylvania project later conducted surface collection and test pits in 1968 and excavation in 1969.

## What archaeologists found

Researchers recorded low platforms interpreted as possible house foundations, abundant surface pottery and a boulder carved with four human figures.

## Research history

### 2020 — Historical synthesis

A review of Chalchuapa fieldwork identifies 1968 surface collection and test pits followed by excavation at Finca Las Victorias in 1969.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 1969 — Fieldwork

The Pennsylvania project excavated at Finca Las Victorias during a five-month season that also included El Trapiche, Casa Blanca and Laguna Cuzcachapa.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 1968 — Fieldwork

The project conducted surface collection and test pits before the following year's excavations. The summary does not report site-specific results.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### December 1942–February 1943 — Field exploration

A Museo Nacional expedition conducted explorations, but the source says no definitive report was available.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/longyear#Page 71|longyear.pdf, PDF p. 71 (printed p. 69), note 20]]

### By 1942 — Field survey

The reconnaissance inventory described the platforms, surface pottery and carved boulder and stated that no excavation had been done.

**Evidence:** [[Papers/longyear#Page 24|longyear.pdf, PDF p. 24]]
