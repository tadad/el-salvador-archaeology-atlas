# Las Victorias, Chalchuapa

## Description

A group of low platforms and abundant surface pottery on the former Finca Las Victorias, east of Chalchuapa. Pre-Hispanic, otherwise undated in the cited source. Researchers recorded low platforms interpreted as possible house foundations, abundant surface pottery and a boulder carved with four human figures. [[Papers/longyear#Page 24|Source: PDF p. 24 (printed p. 17), "Las Victorias Group"; PDF p. 71 (printed p. 69), note 20]]

## Studies

### 2020: Historical synthesis

A review of Chalchuapa fieldwork identifies 1968 surface collection and test pits followed by excavation at Finca Las Victorias in 1969. [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|Source: 2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 1969: Fieldwork

The Pennsylvania project excavated at Finca Las Victorias during a five-month season that also included El Trapiche, Casa Blanca and Laguna Cuzcachapa. [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|Source: 2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### 1968: Fieldwork

The project conducted surface collection and test pits before the following year's excavations. The summary does not report site-specific results. [[Papers/2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019#Page 7|Source: 2020-flores-investigaciones-el-trapiche-1953-2019.pdf, PDF p. 7 (printed p. 43)]]

### December 1942–February 1943: Field exploration

A Museo Nacional expedition conducted explorations, but the source says no definitive report was available. [[Papers/longyear#Page 71|Source: longyear.pdf, PDF p. 71 (printed p. 69), note 20]]

### By 1942: Field survey

The reconnaissance inventory described the platforms, surface pottery and carved boulder and stated that no excavation had been done. Researchers recorded low platforms interpreted as possible house foundations, abundant surface pottery and a boulder carved with four human figures.
