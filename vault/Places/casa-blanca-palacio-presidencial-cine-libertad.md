# Former Casa Blanca / Palacio Presidencial

## Description

Excavation at the former Casa Blanca or Palacio Presidencial, later occupied by Cine Libertad, exposed twelve ceramic pipes belonging to an imported water-supply system installed after 1870. A maker's mark links at least one pipe to Gladding McBean & Co. of California. [[Papers/2024-flores-riqueza-arqueologica-centro-san-salvador#Page 4|Source: PDF p. 4, Cine Libertad among documented urban excavations]] [Source: Ministry of Culture, 17 January 2023](https://www.cultura.gob.sv/un-tubo-ceramico-de-mas-de-cien-anos-es-la-pieza-del-mes-del-muna-2/)

## Studies

### 2013: Excavation

The excavation documented twelve pipes that supplied the nineteenth-century palace. The Ministry later selected one as a MUNA “Pieza del Mes” and published the archaeological and manufacturing context.
