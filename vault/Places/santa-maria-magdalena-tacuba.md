# Santa María Magdalena de Tacuba

## Description

Santa María Magdalena de Tacuba is a ruined colonial church facing Tacuba’s central park. The report says the first church was built about 1705, was furnished by 1769, and collapsed in the 1773 Santa Marta earthquake. The report records surviving lateral walls and the former sacristy and baptistery rooms.
