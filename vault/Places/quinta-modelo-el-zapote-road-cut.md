# Quinta Modelo–El Zapote road cut

## Description

A road-cut findspot in Barrio San Jacinto where Lardé observed pottery, a worked obsidian fragment, bone, and charcoal in dark soil beneath four to five meters of white volcanic deposits. He located it on a recently opened north–south road between Quinta Modelo and Cuartel El Zapote. The historical road alignment is unresolved, so this locality is kept off the atlas and should not be merged automatically with the later excavation at [[Places/cerro-zapote-san-jacinto|Cerro Zapote]]. [[Papers/arqueologia-cuzcatleca-valle-de-san-salvador#Page 7|Source: PDF pp. 7–8]]

## Studies

### 2024: Historical synthesis

Flores Manzano treats Lardé's observation and subsequent work around El Zapote as part of a widespread pre-Tierra Blanca Joven occupation across metropolitan San Salvador rather than a single buried city. [[Papers/2024-flores-riqueza-arqueologica-centro-san-salvador#Page 3|Source: 2024-flores-riqueza-arqueologica-centro-san-salvador.pdf, PDF p. 3]]

### June 1917: Road-cut observation

Lardé examined the exposed stratigraphy and collected archaeological material from the buried dark soil. This was observation of a construction cut, not a controlled excavation.
