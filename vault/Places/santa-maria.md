# Santa María

## Description

A Guazapa-phase settlement in the Cerrón Grande reservoir, with a principal pyramid and an I-shaped ballcourt. Most of the site is underwater at higher reservoir levels, and severe erosion was documented in 2003. Early Postclassic, about 900–1200 CE. The 1976 plan showed a principal pyramid and an I-shaped ballcourt on a large artificial platform. [[Papers/drowned#Page 3|Source: PDF pp. 3–5 (printed pp. 1–3), introduction and previous studies]]

## Studies

### May 2003: Condition assessment

Investigators measured and photographed exposed remains, prepared a new sketch, and assessed inundation damage. A 2003 low-water visit recorded exposed remains with GPS, measurements, and photographs. [[Papers/drowned#Page 1|Source: drowned.pdf, PDF pp. 1–5, 23–27]]

### March 2002: Condition assessment

The inundated site was revisited to observe its condition. [[Papers/drowned#Page 1|Source: drowned.pdf, PDF pp. 1–5]]

### 1976: Fieldwork

Fifteen structures were mapped and a structure and several test units were excavated. The 1976 plan showed a principal pyramid and an I-shaped ballcourt on a large artificial platform. [[Papers/drowned#Page 3|Source: drowned.pdf, PDF pp. 3–5]]

### 1975: Site recording

Survey recognized neighboring structural groups as parts of one site. [[Papers/drowned#Page 3|Source: drowned.pdf, PDF pp. 3–4]]

### 1974: Site recording

Boggs registered the locality as Las Lajas. [[Papers/drowned#Page 3|Source: drowned.pdf, PDF pp. 3–4]]
