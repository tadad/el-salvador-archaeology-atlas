# Quebrada Las Marías

## Description

A surveyed lithic locality near Quebrada Las Marías, about 1 km east of the main road south of Perquín. The site was visited in 1954; the cited report does not date the lithic material. Investigators recovered more than 100 obsidian pieces, including eight small points and small end scrapers. ## Studies

### 29 June–2 July 1954: Fieldwork

Haberland visited the locality during the Morazán reconnaissance and recorded archaeological material. During the June 29–July 2 reconnaissance, artifacts were recovered from wash cuts and from soil as deep as 10 cm. No controlled excavation is documented. Investigators recovered more than 100 obsidian pieces, including eight small points and small end scrapers. The field notes did not include descriptions or drawings of these objects.
