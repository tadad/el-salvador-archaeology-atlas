# Source-Derived Research Leads

Wikipedia coverage is uneven, especially for rescue archaeology, artifact
provenance, local rock art, and unpublished field reports. This register captures
subjects revealed directly by the downloaded source collections. Evidence labels
are provisional and apply only to the core described in the cited source.

specific pre-Hispanic belief require separate demonstration.
